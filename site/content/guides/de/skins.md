Skins change more than appearance. The game has 58 of them and 36 carry bonuses, with the best one granting up to 5.5%, comparable to a whole research branch. They attach to different things, from the city and the squad to the march, the raven and the avatar frame, so the percentages stack from several places at once.

Many skins come from events and rankings: avatar frames from titles, the Eagle Castle from the alliance league, seasonal skins from the Age. A noticeable share of these percentages is earned by taking part rather than by paying, and a missed season carries them away.

Duration deserves a separate look. Many skins are granted for 7, 15 or 30 days, and the bonus leaves when the skin does.

## The Black Raven Fortress

The Black Raven Fortress city skin belongs to the group that gives two bonuses at once: 1% damage to monsters stays for good from the moment it is obtained, and another 5% applies while the skin is equipped. It comes as items for 7, 15, 30 and 60 days and as a permanent one, all under the same name, so the duration has to be read in the item description. No shop in the game sells it: the button on the skin leads to the events window, and events and bundles are the only way to get it.
