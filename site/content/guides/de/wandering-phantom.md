Wandering Phantom is an event on the world map, and on its day Wandering Blights attack territories more often than usual. Participation opens at Sanctuary level 8. The first-kill reward for a Blight is paid up to 3 times that day instead of once, the whole Kingdom closes kill quests together, and after the event every player receives a mailed reward for their rank by total damage. The same Blight is fought by the alliance in the Hunt Battle, there in waves on its own territory.

## How the Blights roam

Wandering Blights spawn on the map every 10 minutes, with no more than 70 on the map at once. Each one lives for 4 hours and leaves on its own if it is not killed. The Blights come in 10 levels, from 100 to 190, and the recommended Might for a rally grows from 1,393,000 to 34,311,000. The table shows what a rally against each level drops.

A Blight breaking into a territory during the event does not relocate the city: it stays in place, and idle soldiers die at a reduced rate. Defense still needs preparing, because attacks come more often on the event day, and rallying someone else's Blight for the reward does not replace guarding one's own territory.
