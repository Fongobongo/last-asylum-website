## Tools

Hall of Honor stands as a memorial and takes no part in hero progression, since Honor levels are opened by the faction statues. The Honor screen is entered through those statues rather than through the hall. The building is available from the start of the game and goes up to level 1. Only one can be built.

## Full upgrade

Taking the building from level 1 to 1 costs Grain 12, Timber 9. Pure build time without speed-ups: under a minute.

This building gives no stat bonus: it unlocks an option rather than percentages.

## How to use it

The Hall of Honor costs 12 Grain and 9 Timber, goes up in seconds, and ends there: one level, no upgrades, no stat bonuses. All it gives the city is 500 Might.

The price of that Might shows how cheap the building is. The Soldier's Rest reaches the same 500 Might only at its fifth level, and that costs 33,326 resources and 19 minutes of construction. The Residence brings 100 for 840 resources, a fifth of what the hall carries. Four instant buildings in the city are worth 500 Might, and two of them, the Private Stable and the Alliance Stable, wait for Sanctuary level 11.

Honor itself passes the hall by. Honor levels are opened by a faction statue raised to level 20, the branch runs to 600 levels and 15,030 shards of a single hero, and the HP bonus climbs from 160 at the first level to 240,000 at the six hundredth. The Honor screen is entered through the statue, and the Warrior Statue opens at Sanctuary level 7 while the Ranger Statue waits until level 12.

The hall therefore goes up on day one together with the Temple: 42 resources for the pair, 1,000 Might, and not a single day of queue. The hall is never returned to, and against the 384,300 Might of a fully raised Sanctuary its 500 shows only in the first week.
