## Tools

Warlock Statue

Warlock Statue strengthens every hero of the Warlock faction at once: each level of the statue adds HP, ATK, DEF and command to all of them. The building goes up to level 30, and the bonus grows with every step, so the statue of the faction that makes up the main squad pays off faster than any other building with hero bonuses. On top of that, at level 20 the statue unlocks the shards of the heroes of its faction: without it, heroes of that faction cannot be recruited.

## How to use it

Sanctuary level 11 unlocks three buildings, two of them instant, the Private Stable for 10 Grain and the Alliance Stable for 21 resources. The Warlock Statue is the only one that occupies the queue, and it occupies it for a long while: 299 days 4 hours, 1,253,641,685 Grain and 3,784,783,300 Timber up to level 30.

The game has nine Warlocks, five of them UR, against fourteen Warriors. The roster is shorter, yet the level 30 bonus is identical across all three statues: 151,500 HP, 723 ATK and 723 DEF, and 100 CMD for every hero of the faction, including those kept in reserve.

Level 20 stands apart. It opens the Honor levels of the Warlocks and is reached after 13 days of building. That step alone costs 14,250,000 Grain and 42,760,000 Timber, and before Sanctuary level 11 the statue does not stand in the city at all. While the statue sits below level 20 the shards of a Warlock at the full fifty stars have nowhere to go, and Honor spends those same shards, 15,030 of them for all 600 levels.

The statue carries 272,600 Might, the highest of the three faction statues, but its lead over the Warrior Statue and its 272,400 is under a tenth of a percent. A statue is picked by the make-up of the main squad rather than by these figures. A full upgrade also spends 832,324,200 Herb, and the last five levels take 79% of the whole build time.
