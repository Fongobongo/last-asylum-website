Special events live in the Special Event tab on the city screen, on the right next to the Value Event button. Unlike the bonus events, which pay for purchases and for logging in, this tab holds contests and battles: personal, alliance-wide and server-wide. The tab appears at the first Sanctuary levels, and its contents grow with the age of the server, with some events arriving only in the third week.

## What the tab holds

Version 1.0.87 shows nine events in the tab, listed below in the order the game uses.
- 서바이벌 배틀: five daily phases, and only the actions of the running phase score: hero recruitment, construction, training, research and the Raven.
- 수정 골짜기: a separate map that opens for half an hour, where alliances mine Purified Crystal and seize clusters from each other.
- 협곡 대전: an alliance battle across three lanes with no soldier losses, six alliances to a group and five rounds.
- 방랑하는 섀도우: a day when Wandering Blights roam the map more often and the first-kill reward is paid three times.
- 치즈 함정: an alliance boss caught in a trap for half an hour and hit with rallies, with difficulty rising by server day.
- 좀비 포위전: waves of Blights from a prison against the territories of the whole alliance, and the main source of antitoxin.
- 지혜 퀴즈: fifteen questions, with diamonds paid even for a wrong answer.
- 계정 연동 혜택: the window that links the game account to a platform account and pays a reward for the link.
- 마지막 여명: a starting reward track for total Might, eleven thresholds in a row.

## When each one arrives

Every event has a Sanctuary level from which it shows, and some also have a server day: the 기념비 timeline in the city announces in advance on which day an event comes. For events that follow a weekly schedule, the day depends on the weekday the server opened, and the table gives the range for them.

서바이벌 배틀 and 치즈 함정 come first, while the Valley and the Canyon only arrive in the third week, once alliances have members to send onto the map. 좀비 포위전 sits on day fifteen, yet it is started by the alliance itself after gathering clues on Falcon Quests, not by the calendar.
