## Why she matters

Role: A ranger DPS who spreads her damage across several targets at once and lives on critical hits. Lunar Hunt seizes three random targets for 1,800% of ATK each, while the ultimate Moonfall Dance covers an area for 2,976% and raises her critical hit chance, which the passive Moon's Blessing raises again.

Worth investing: 신시아 ranks third of sixteen DPS heroes by maximum Might (916,153) and sits in tier A. She opens up as early as server day 7, and her 99 guaranteed free shards are more than any UR hero except 말레나, which makes her the realistic second UR for a player who buys nothing. She is worth levelling as soon as she is obtained.

## How to play

신시아 strikes more often rather than harder. Her ultimate Moonfall Dance gives 2,976% of ATK, the smallest figure among UR DPS heroes: 광대 reaches 5,047.5%, 레드 레이디 4,550.4% and 조야 3,669.6%. Lunar Hunt, however, seizes three targets for 1,800% each every 5 seconds, and 신시아 raises her critical chance twice, by 16% with the ultimate and by 24% with the passive skill.

She opens up on server day 7, earlier than any other UR DPS hero, and her Awakening tab arrives on season day 15, the second one after 말레나. Her guaranteed free shards number 99, more than any UR hero except 말레나, and she stands in the guarantee of the third recruit pool.

Awakening costs the most. For 2,870 shards it gives 1,490,016 HP and 18,952 DEF, more than the 1,113,223 from 150 levels and nearly five times more than the 318,010 from all 975 shards spent on stars. For 조야 and 광대 the same system adds only 425,119 HP.

In a tier A squad 신시아 stands with 아서, 하퍼, 루이스 and 레드 레이디 for 123.7M effective HP and 87 out of 100. She is a ranger, so a five with 섀도우, 루이스, 레드 레이디 and 벨 earns her the +20% bonus to stats, the largest squad multiplier in the game.
