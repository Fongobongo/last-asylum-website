## Tools

Watchtower hides intel from enemy scouts, and from level 5 counter-scouting triggers every single time. From level 9 five fields drop out of the enemy report, and neither figure grows further. The Expedition is entered through the tower. The building unlocks after Sanctuary level 8 and goes up to level 30. Only one can be built. Levels raise: anti-Scout Chance, anti-Scout Hidden Intel, scout Level.

## Full upgrade

Taking the building from level 1 to 30 costs Grain 3,716,654,672, Timber 3,716,654,672, Herb 476,355,800. Pure build time without speed-ups: 350d 6h, that is, about 12 months.

## How to use it

In pure build time the Watchtower yields only to the Sanctuary and the Walls: 350 days 6 hours against 398 days for both of them. Yet almost everything the tower is built for arrives within the first day.

At level 1 counter-scouting catches an enemy scout in one case out of five, from level 5 it works every time, and the number of fields hidden from the enemy report reaches five at level 9. That whole stretch costs about 27 hours of building and 3,052,672 each of Grain and Timber.

Beyond it twenty one levels raise nothing but the level of the city's own scout, from 2 to 22, and they take the remaining 349 days out of 350. That level grows by one step per tower level starting from the tenth. Counter-scouting does not grow across them, so the tower is kept at level 9 for as long as the city holds buildings with a quicker return. All thirty levels cost 3,716,654,672 Grain, the same amount of Timber and 476,355,800 Herb.

The Expedition is entered through the tower, yet the Expedition itself appears at Sanctuary level 16, eight levels later than the tower. Its 311,100 Might puts the Watchtower fifth among the city buildings, and that figure is collected by the same levels that add nothing to scouting.
