Special events live in the Special Event tab on the city screen, on the right next to the Value Event button. Unlike the bonus events, which pay for purchases and for logging in, this tab holds contests and battles: personal, alliance-wide and server-wide. The tab appears at the first Sanctuary levels, and its contents grow with the age of the server, with some events arriving only in the third week.

## What the tab holds

Version 1.0.87 shows nine events in the tab, listed below in the order the game uses.
- Batalha de Sobrevivência: five daily phases, and only the actions of the running phase score: hero recruitment, construction, training, research and the Raven.
- Vale do Cristal Aglomerado: a separate map that opens for half an hour, where alliances mine Purified Crystal and seize clusters from each other.
- Conquista do Cânion: an alliance battle across three lanes with no soldier losses, six alliances to a group and five rounds.
- Fantasma Errante: a day when Wandering Blights roam the map more often and the first-kill reward is paid three times.
- Armadilha de Queijo: an alliance boss caught in a trap for half an hour and hit with rallies, with difficulty rising by server day.
- Cerco dos Mortos-Vivos: waves of Blights from a prison against the territories of the whole alliance, and the main source of antitoxin.
- Quiz da Sabedoria: fifteen questions, with diamonds paid even for a wrong answer.
- Vincular para Recompensa: the window that links the game account to a platform account and pays a reward for the link.
- Amanhecer Final: a starting reward track for total Might, eleven thresholds in a row.

## When each one arrives

Every event has a Sanctuary level from which it shows, and some also have a server day: the Monumento timeline in the city announces in advance on which day an event comes. For events that follow a weekly schedule, the day depends on the weekday the server opened, and the table gives the range for them.

Batalha de Sobrevivência and Armadilha de Queijo come first, while the Valley and the Canyon only arrive in the third week, once alliances have members to send onto the map. Cerco dos Mortos-Vivos sits on day fifteen, yet it is started by the alliance itself after gathering clues on Falcon Quests, not by the calendar.
