Undead Siege is the alliance defense event: your whole alliance stands against **waves of infected**, with heroes on the wall doing the fighting. Two rules define it before anything else: **shields do not work**, and it is **not a points event** — the goal is to defend as many waves as possible.

This guide covers how sieges unlock, the difficulty ladder, the hidden multi-squad mechanic that wipes underprepared alliances, rewards, and the top seven mistakes.

## How a Siege Unlocks {#unlocking}

Sieges are not on a timer — your alliance triggers them (the event becomes available to Alliances **2 weeks after server launch** — July 9, 2026 update):

1. Fill the **300,000 Clue Points** bar.
2. An **R4 or R5 finds the hidden Death Prison** on the map.
3. That leader **starts the siege and chooses the difficulty**.
4. The siege begins roughly **4 hours after the click** — use that gap to get every member's heroes on the wall.

Clue points come from normal daily play:

- Attacking **Rebels** and the **Blight Legion**
- Killing **Wandering Blights**
- Gathering resources on tiles
- Helping allies (help-build and help-research taps)
- Running **Alliance Excavation**
- Completing **Falcon Quests**

An active alliance fills the bar through ordinary play; the bar is not the bottleneck, readiness is.

## Difficulty Levels {#difficulty}

| Difficulty | Enemy Might | Territory |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | 2.4M | Territory 13 |
| Level 6 | ~3 squads per wave | — |
| Level 10 | 24.5M | Territory 27 |

The ladder scales continuously from 2.4M Enemy Might at Level 1 up to 24.5M at Level 10, with territory requirements rising from 13 to 27. Higher difficulty levels also unlock **Enemy Mutations** — **preview the mutations before committing** to a difficulty, because a mutation that counters your defense comp is worth millions of Enemy Might.

## The Hidden Multi-Squad Mechanic {#hidden-mechanic}

This is the part of the event the difficulty screen does not tell you, and it is the difference between surviving 20 waves and collapsing at 17:

- The siege sends **2 or more squads at the stated might**. Stated 8M means you fight two squads of 8M — effectively double the number on the button.
- At **wave 17** of an 8M difficulty, the actual strength is roughly **7.3M x 2**.
- At **difficulty 6 and above, waves send 3 squads**.
- **Squad HP persists between waves.** Your tank starts the next fight at whatever HP it ended the last one — and your squad 1 must survive **back-to-back fights** with no heal in between.

> A 9.5M player died at wave 17 of an 8M difficulty — worn down by consecutive fights with persistent HP. The stated might is not the fight.

## Rewards {#rewards}

### Personal Milestones {#personal-rewards}

Each player faces **20 solo waves**, with milestones at waves **1, 4, 8, 10, 13, 16 and 20** — tokens, upgrades and chests. If your city falls it will **not be attacked again**, but your personal rewards stop there: dying at wave 12 of 20 forfeits every milestone after it.

### Alliance Milestones {#alliance-rewards}

| Alliance total waves | Rewards |
|---|---|
| 50 | Hero XP, materials, medals |
| 100 | Hero XP, materials, medals |
| 150 | Hero XP, materials, medals |
| 200 | Hero XP, materials, medals |
| 250 | Hero XP, materials, medals |
| 300 | Hero XP, materials, medals |

Two rules around the alliance total:

- **Membership over 48 hours** is required to receive alliance rewards — new joins don't collect.
- **Reinforcing other members doesn't double your personal rewards** — it raises the alliance total. Do it anyway: the alliance milestones are the big payout.

The all-time record stands at **1,193 waves** in a single siege.

## Deployment: Squads and Reinforcing {#deployment}

- **Your strongest squad goes on your own wall.** That squad carries your 20 personal waves.
- **The rest of your squads reinforce other members** — reinforcing raises the alliance total, which is where the shared rewards live.
- Reinforcements land at the member's wall and fight the incoming waves with them — send them before the waves hit, not after the notification.

## The Top 7 Mistakes {#top-mistakes}

1. **Heroes not on the wall** — the biggest killer. Heroes on the wall are the event; a wall with no heroes is a city waiting to fall.
2. **Ignoring wall defense** — there is **no auto-defend** in this event. Whatever you preset is what fights.
3. **Starting without 100% clue completion** — a rushed start wastes the 4-hour warning window.
4. **Underestimating mutations** — always read the mutation preview before choosing the difficulty.
5. **Not reinforcing members** — the alliance milestones need bodies on every wall, not just your own.
6. **Starting with partial squads** — a half-empty wall squad is a dead wall squad.
7. **Logging off without preset defense** — offline defense works, but **only if it was pre-set**. Set it, then sleep.

Run the event with those seven fixed and your alliance defends near its ceiling. Hero setup for the wall and squad composition are covered in the [Heroes guide](/heroes/).
