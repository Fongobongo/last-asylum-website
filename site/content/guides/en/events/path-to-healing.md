The **Path to Healing** is a critical 5-day onboarding event for every new survivor in Last Asylum: Plague. Completing its mission track guarantees **15 UR Arthur Hero Fragments** — the undisputed top frontline tank for early-to-mid game — and **30 SSR Celia Fragments**, securing a resilient backbone for your primary squad.

However, the event features a strict, non-negotiable eligibility prerequisite that blindsides many beginners: **the VIP Level Gate**.

---

## 🔐 MANDATORY REQUIREMENT: Unlock VIP 5 {#vip-requirement}

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PREREQUISITE:** To claim your earned Path to Healing completion rewards, your account **MUST reach VIP Level 5 before the event concludes!**

Even if you clear 100% of the daily mission objectives across all 5 days, failing to reach VIP 5 means **all milestone rewards and Arthur fragments remain locked!**

### How to reach VIP 5 as a 100% F2P player:
1. **Never waste early diamonds:** The game showers new players with thousands of free diamonds via Campaign chapters, Sanctuary level milestones, and first-time achievements.
2. **Invest directly into VIP Points:** Open your VIP menu (top-left avatar area) and spend free diamonds directly to purchase VIP experience points.
3. **Open all Alliance Gift Chests:** Every chest opened by your alliance members drops supplementary VIP points.

---

## 📅 Day-by-Day Mission Schedule (Days 1–5) {#daily-tasks}

Tasks unlock sequentially on a daily cadence, but all previously unlocked days remain active and completable until the final event deadline:

### 🟡 Day 1
- **Total Logins:** Daily server check-in.
- **Shrine Upgrades:** Elevating Sanctuary level to specified milestones.
- **Adventure Actions:** Clearing map exploration nodes and story chapters.

### 🟡 Day 2
- **Raven Tower:** Conquering floors in the Raven Tower trial gauntlet.
- **Arena:** Executing duels in the Arena of Fate.
- **Heroes:** Leveling up heroes and ascending star ratings.

### 🟡 Day 3
- **Train Troops:** Mobilizing units across your military barracks.
- **Training Arena:** Engaging specialized training modules.
- **Guild Assistance:** Sending alliance help clicks (handshake icon) to comrades.

### 🟡 Day 4
- **Research:** Finalizing technology nodes in the Research Laboratory.
- **Magic Raven:** Feeding and augmenting the Guardian Raven.
- **Recruit:** Deploying Normal and Advanced Recruitment Tickets in the Tavern.

### 🟡 Day 5
- **Eliminate Ghouls:** Purging roaming infected mobs on the wilderness map.
- **Combat Power (CP):** Attaining specified overall squad combat power thresholds.
- **Speed-ups:** Burning minutes of construction, research, and training speedups.

## 🎁 Reward Roster {#rewards}

- 🧩 **15 UR Arthur Fragments:** Unlocks or accelerates star ascension for the premier defensive powerhouse.
- 🧩 **30 SSR Celia Fragments:** Unlocks Celia, an essential support buffer for campaign and PvE bossing.
- 🎁 **Event Completion Grand Cache:** Packed with high-tier resource crates, speedup bundles, and golden recruit tickets.

---

## 💡 Tactical Completion Tips {#pro-tips}

1. **Avoid the Last-Day Crunch:** Long-timer missions like Technology Research (Day 4), Troop Mobilization (Day 3), and Raven Tower floors (Day 2) take hours. Queue them up well before Day 5!
2. **Audit your VIP 5 progress on Day 1:** Calculate your remaining VIP point deficit immediately and allocate free diamonds daily to hit VIP 5 comfortably ahead of schedule.
3. **Stay active with your Alliance:** Respond to every alliance assistance prompt — it's the fastest, completely free path to completing Day 3's assistance tasks.
