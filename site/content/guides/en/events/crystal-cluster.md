**Crystal Cluster Valley** is a timed battle-royale-style event: you spend a fixed session on a shared map killing red neutral mobs and mining crystals, and your score — not kills on other players — decides the reward bracket. F2P and whales start the map on equal footing; discipline beats wallet.

## The Scoreboard Maths {#scoring}

Three sources of points matter:

| Source | Points | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Red neutral mobs** ("reds") | **~1,500 each** | Instant, repeatable, and respawn dense. The backbone of every F2P run. |
| **Crystal mines** (attacking player-held Crystal nodes) | **~5,000 per minute held** | Explosive if you can sustain occupation; risky if you overextend. |
| Personal skill unlocks / gear boosts | multiplicative | Passive amplifiers on everything above. |

Rack up reds early, pivot to mines once your skills are high, and you'll outscore most casual whales.

## The F2P Route {#route}

1. **Scout the map first.** Before attacking anything, zoom out and locate the densest cluster of red mobs near your spawn. The right starting spot saves 5 minutes of marching every cycle.
2. **Farm reds relentlessly at ~1,500 pts per kill.** Cluster them: park your march so each kill chains into the next with near-zero travel time. Korpez' rule: if the march bar ever idles, you chose the wrong spot.
3. **Unlock your skill tree immediately.** The Crystal event skill panel opens on the side of the screen — dump points into red-kill bonuses, then movement speed, then mine bonuses. Do not wait; every minute of delay is lost multiplier time.
4. **Use a stopwatch / timer app for mine windows.** Hitting an occupied mine pays ~5K per minute you hold; pre-set a timer for the payout ticks so you can grab mines at the moment your opponents rotate off them.
5. **Only then contest mines.** Once your reds are cleared or your skills are maxed, jump onto a contested mine mid-cycle — ideally one whose owner just left for another red pack.

## Discipline Rules {#rules}

- **Never idle.** Twenty-five minutes of continuous reds > 35 minutes of wandering.
- **Don't fight players early.** PvP duels feed points to your opponent and stall your green-scoring runs. Pivot away; come back later.
- **Don't chase a contested mine solo.** You'll invest 60s and get 5K — meanwhile three red kills would have paid 4.5K per 90s with zero risk. Only hold mines when uncontested or heavily stacked.
- **Skills are not optional.** The skill points won't farm themselves — open that panel the second you see it.

## The Whale Counter {#whales}

Paying players mostly buy march-speed packs and stronger skills faster. The answer is not to race their speed — it's to **out-discipline** them: hit more reds per minute, never waste a second fighting them, and steal the mine they just cleared the moment they leave. Most whales plateau at 5K mines; a clean 30-reds-per-minute F2P run lands the same bracket.
