The **Strike First** event is the premier onboarding sprint for new servers and starting commanders in Last Asylum: Plague. Triggered immediately upon account creation, it serves as your primary growth accelerator during Week 1: completing foundational milestones awards an abundance of free diamonds, speedups, recruit tickets, and hero shards.

---

## 🎯 Event Overview & Task Structure {#overview}

Strike First functions as a tiered milestone checklist tied directly to your Sanctuary progression and early-game combat preparedness:

1. **Sanctuary Milestones:**
   - Upgrading your Sanctuary to Lv.5, 8, 10, 12, and 15.
   - Each milestone unlocks massive caches of free diamonds, building speedups, and essential resources.

2. **Blight Purge (Monster Hunts):**
   - Eliminating roaming undead mutants and Blight Walkers on the world map.
   - Yields research speedups, Raven essence, and hospital recovery supplies.

3. **Military Mobilization:**
   - Training tier 1, 2, and 3 troops (T1–T3).
   - Expanding barracks capacity and unlocking additional march slots.

4. **Tavern Recruitment:**
   - Spending Normal and Advanced Recruitment Tickets.
   - Unlocking foundational heroes (Celia, Bella, Arthur).

5. **Arena of Fate Debut:**
   - Participating in server-wide arena duels to establish an early PvP ranking.

---

## 🎁 Milestone Rewards {#rewards}

Full completion of Strike First delivers game-changing starter value:
- **Diamonds:** 5,000+ free diamonds.
- **Advanced Recruitment Tickets:** 20+ tickets for Tavern summons.
- **Hero Shards:** Fragments for UR Arthur (tank) and SSR Celia (support).
- **Speedup Bundles:** Tens of hours in universal, building, and research speedups.
- **Antitoxin Reserves:** Crucial serum to elevate your core carry's level immediately.

## ⚠️ 4 Fatal Beginner Mistakes to Avoid {#mistakes}

### 1. Blowing Free Diamonds on Standard Tavern Pulls
The single biggest mistake new players make is dumping free diamonds into standard recruit pulls. **Do not do this.** On Day 8 of your server, the UR Ranger **Cynthia's Lucky Wheel** arrives — a strong early-carry pickup requiring banked diamonds (~11,500 for a copy). Only pull using free tickets; hoard diamonds for the Wheel and VIP progression.

### 2. Disorganized Construction Queues
Do not waste building queues on vanity upgrades or unnecessary resource plots. Always inspect the prerequisite buildings for the next Sanctuary level (typically Wall + Research Lab or Wall + Barracks) and upgrade **strictly along the critical path**.

### 3. Wasting Stamina Outside Event Windows
Only hunt the specific monster tiers required to trigger milestone completions. Conserve remaining stamina for double-dipping opportunities.

### 4. Premature PvP Raiding
Attacking neighboring player bases during Week 1 fills your Infirmary with wounded troops, consumes valuable healing food, and stalls Sanctuary upgrades. Keep your peace shield active and focus entirely on PvE development.

## 📋 Quick Start Checklist {#checklist}

1. Unlock the second builder queue immediately.
2. Maintain 24/7 research queues focused on the "Base Development" tree.
3. Keep barracks constantly training troops in manageable batches.
4. Join an active Top-5 alliance: member assistance taps shave off hours of construction timers.
5. Claim all Strike First milestone rewards before the event timer expires.
