The **Pandemic Experience** is a recurring bi-weekly combat trial: you fight waves of "Hell Nurses" of rising difficulty — **Level 1 through Level 9** — and both you *and your alliance* climb together. The deeper your alliance goes, the more bosses open up. Rewards scale with difficulty: UR Omni Shards, Raven Essence, and diamonds.

## How Progression Works {#progression}

- You start at **Difficulty 1**. Beat it → Difficulty 2 unlocks. The event returns roughly **every 2 weeks**, so you climb one or two levels per cycle.
- **Level 9** is the wall — squad power requirements get steep (~tens of millions of Might).
- **Alliance milestones:** the next boss unlocks when enough members (e.g. **40 players**) clear current top difficulty. If you stop pushing, you stall the whole alliance.
- **Records tab** shows where every member stands — use it to see who needs a push.

## Rewards {#rewards}

- **UR Omni Shards** (the awakening currency)
- **Raven Essence** (the permanent Raven bottleneck)
- Diamonds and speedups: nice, but they're the cherry, not the cake

Climb as high as you can every cycle: reward volume scales with the difficulty reached.

## Beating High Difficulty: Pro Tips {#tips}

### 1. Swap UR gauntlets for SSR gauntlets
Same [World Boss trick](/heroes/): SSR gloves carry **bonus damage vs monsters** which matters more than raw UR ATK on PvE targets. Swap before starting.

### 2. Enable War Frenzy first
Scout any player → triggers the **War Frenzy buff** (extra attack) that also applies in this event. Costs you one scout, saves you wounded troops.

### 3. The Weak-Hero Rally Trick
Your own squads hit the wall at high difficulties? Use rallies to borrow teammates' power:

1. Empty a slot: put **one weak, low-level hero** alone in a rally squad.
2. Launch **3 rallies at the same time** at 3 nurses.
3. Strong ally players (whales/spenders) join and annihilate the nurse.
4. Your squad takes minimal losses — your Infirmary stays empty.

~10 minutes and 2–3 weak heroes is all it costs.

### 4. Coordinate the timing
Agree in alliance chat on a shared window: everyone pushes, whales are online to absorb rallies, and 40-member checklists get cleared within an evening.

## The Only Real Mistake {#mistake}

**Stopping mid-ladder.** A player parked at Difficulty 7 while the alliance sits at 9 is dragging everyone's unlock progress. Push every cycle — even with rallies and helpers, your difficulty count is what the alliance gates on.
