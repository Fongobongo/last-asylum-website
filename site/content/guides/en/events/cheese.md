Cheese Trap is an **alliance PvE rally event** against a giant rat. Your personal damage earns personal rewards across five phases, and the alliance's total damage earns separate alliance rewards. It looks like a pure power event — it is actually a planning event, and the difference is worth several reward tiers.

This guide covers the two reward tracks, the stop-point rule, level scaling, and how to read the rankings before your alliance commits to a rat.

## How It Works {#how-it-works}

- An **R4 or R5 sets the start** via the unified Event Schedule. From that click the alliance has **about 1.5 hours to attack**.
- **Cursed Land Placement Forbidden:** Since the July 2, 2026 update, placing new Cheese Traps in the Cursed Land is no longer permitted.
- **Cheese Trap I and II differ only in time** — same rat, same rules, different window. Pick whichever fits your schedule.
- **You can only fight ONE of the two rats.** There is no splitting your troops across both.
- **Offline participation works** — join the rally ahead of time and your squads fight even if you log off. Set your march before the window opens, then live your life.

## The Two Reward Tracks {#two-reward-tracks}

| Track | Based on | Rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Personal | Your own damage | 5 phases of escalating rewards |
| Alliance | Alliance total damage | Alliance-wide reward tiers |

The interaction between the tracks is the important part:

- **STOP after personal Phase 5.** Damage past that point is pure troop loss — zero extra personal rewards for the troops you burn.
- **Both rats share one HP pool for alliance rewards.** If one player reaches Phase 5 on one rat and another stops at Phase 3 on the other, the alliance total counts as if everyone hit **Phase 5**. The pool — not the phase — is what matters.

## Rally Mechanics: What Actually Matters {#rally-mechanics}

- **Joining an R4/R5 rally grants +2.5% attack.** Join the rally instead of solo-marching. Every time.
- **Rally initiator strength and join order have ZERO effect on your damage.** Joining first does not hit harder; joining last does not leech. Stop jockeying for position — it changes nothing.
- **Every troop deals damage.** The rat's HP pool is enormous, and no single heavy hitter can "use it up" — a full march of cheap troops out-damages a half march of expensive ones. Bring bodies, not just elites.

## Level Scaling: Which Rat to Fight {#level-scaling}

The level decision has a clean rule:

> Phase 4 of the next-level rat is marginally better than Phase 5 of the previous level — and pays better total rewards.

In practice:

- Established alliances run **level 7 or 8**.
- **Near a KvK day, pick level 7** — you want troops alive for the [war](/events/kvk/), and the marginal reward of level 8 is not worth the extra losses.

Before committing, **check the rankings**: count the players hitting Phase 5 on the level 6 rat versus the players reaching Phase 4+ on level 7. If more of your roster clears P4 on 7 than P5 on 6, the higher rat is the better call.

### Reading the In-Game Rewards Table {#rewards-table}

The full rewards table is in the game: **Special event > Cheese Trap tab > Gift button > Rewards**. Read it before the rat opens — the phase-by-phase comparison tells you exactly where your roster's stopping point lands.

## Team Setup {#team-setup}

Squad priorities for a long boss fight:

- **Survivability + sustained DPS + synergy.** The rat is a marathon, not a burst check.
- **Tanky frontline** — heroes that stand for the full fight.
- **Strong backline** — the damage engine behind the frontline.
- **Support-healer** — keeps the frontline alive, which keeps the backline safe, which keeps damage flowing.

Specific hero picks and faction synergies are in the [Heroes guide](/heroes/).

## The Whale-Carry Option {#whale-carry}

If your alliance has one heavy hitter, there is an efficient endgame pattern: **2-3 rallies are enough to max alliance rewards** when the whale carries the damage. Everyone else joins the rally for the +2.5%, drops their troops in, and stops at their personal Phase 5. The whale's damage fills the shared pool; the alliance tier fills; nobody burns troops past their own stop point.
