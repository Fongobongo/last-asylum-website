Elixir Scramble is a **30-minute alliance-versus-alliance battlefield** where the winner is decided by points, not by who kills more troops. Alliances that treat it like a deathmatch lose to alliances that treat it like a map-control game — every time.

This guide covers the five stages of the event, where the points actually come from, teleport rules, defense logic, and the one mistake that ends a run before it starts.

## The Five Stages {#event-structure}

Every scramble runs the same arc, about six minutes per stage across the half hour:

1. **Matching** — the system pairs alliances.
2. **Entry prep** — you load in, position, and get your squads off the wall (see [the wall warning](#wall-warning) — this stage is where runs are lost).
3. **Early strongholds** — the first buildings unlock and the opening land-grab happens.
4. **Mid decisive battles** — the contested center becomes the point pivot; most of the event is won or lost here.
5. **Late accumulation** — held buildings tick out the final points; the alliance that stayed active to the last second collects the difference.

## Registration and Timing {#registration}

- Players sign up as **Team A or Team B**, or the **R5 signs the alliance up** directly.
- **After registration closes, nobody can be added.** No late joiners, no substitutes — if half your alliance sleeps through sign-up, you fight short-handed.
- **Registration Reminder Mails (July 2026 update):** The game sends automatic reminder mails to alliance officers: 4 hours before deadline for unregistered alliances, and 2 hours before deadline if fewer than 10 members are registered.
- Scheduling example: a scramble that starts at **3PM CST runs at 9PM UK**. Check the event timer in your own timezone and set an alarm — the event rewards being online at the exact start, not five minutes in.

## Where Points Come From {#points}

The core rule, and the one most alliances get wrong: **points come from CONTROL, not from kills.** Killing enemy squads earns nothing directly. Holding buildings earns points every tick. A squad that never fights but stands on a high-value building outperforms a squad that wipes three enemies and stands on nothing.

The building hierarchy:

- **Healing Tents** — the second highest point building for the first 10 minutes. They heal **15 soldiers per 10 seconds**, which keeps your fighting squads on the map instead of walking home. Note: **speedups are useless inside the event** — healing runs on the tent, not on your items.
- **Camps** — spawn later in the event and become the point-gathering targets; grab them when they appear.
- **The central Elixir Castle** — the highest-value point source on the map. This is the **priority target for your strongest players**: secure it and hold it. It is also the most contested, so expect the decisive battles to happen around it.

## Teleport Rules {#teleports}

- **Free teleport has a 2-minute cooldown** — and teleporting **cancels any marches you have out**. Never reposition while your squads are mid-march to a target.
- **Direct Teleport is not allowed** inside the event. You cannot buy your way around the map.
- The standard play: **base your city at the castle** and dispatch marches outward from there. One reposition, then a short walk for everything else — this minimizes the cooldown problem entirely.

## Buildings and Defense Logic {#defense}

Claimed buildings do **not** need a garrison to keep generating points. That leads to a simple rule:

- **Only defend a building if it is actually contested.** Parking squads on everything you own is point-neutral and wastes your army.
- When a building does come under attack, **multiple squads can defend it** — stack the defense rather than feeding squads in one at a time.
- You can also **choose not to defend at all**: let a low-value building go, keep your squads alive, and retake it once the enemy marches elsewhere. Points tick back the moment you re-claim.

## CRITICAL: Remove Squads From Your Wall Before the Start {#wall-warning}

> If your squads are still on your wall when Elixir Scramble begins, you can lose your troops in under 10 minutes — and your event is over for the remaining 20.

Wall squads get caught in the opening wave of attacks, and dead squads inside the event are dead for the full half hour. During the entry-prep stage, the checklist is:

1. Pull every squad off the wall.
2. Confirm your marches are empty and cooldowns are ready.
3. Position at the castle.
4. Then and only then, start dispatching.

## The 30-Minute Game Plan {#winning-strategy}

The winning pattern, condensed:

- **Be online at the start.** The early land-grab of strongholds decides who plays from ahead.
- **Stay active the full 30 minutes.** Points accumulate the entire time; the alliance that checks out at minute 20 donates the last third of the event.
- **Hold, don't overextend.** Secure what you have before opening a third front. A building you hold to the end beats two buildings you briefly touched.
- **Rotate and reinforce.** Squads on contested buildings need fresh bodies; rotate tired squads back through Healing Tents.
- **Prioritize high-point buildings.** The Elixir Castle first, Healing Tents early, Camps when they spawn.
- **Never go solo.** A single squad attacking a defended point dies alone and contributes nothing. Move with your alliance or don't move.

Elixir Scramble rewards discipline over power. A weaker alliance that holds the right buildings and never stops rotating will beat a stronger alliance chasing kills — because kills were never worth anything.

## Official in-game rules (client v1.0.102) {#official-rules}


### Elixir Scramble

Elixir Scramble
As the plague spreads across the world, the most scarce resource is always the cure for diseases. According to ancient texts, mysterious herbs grow in a land that only opens on the night of the Blood Moon.
It is said that a large quantity of elixirs is hidden within the legendary Elixir Castle, and these elixirs can effectively treat various diseases, attracting many to compete for them...


### Entrance Area

Entry Area
Two alliances will enter from the east and west sides of the battlefield, competing for points to achieve ultimate victory.


### Elixir Castle

Elixir Castle
The most crucial structure on the battlefield, located at the center of the area. The castle gates open 10 minutes into the battle, granting substantial points upon capture.


### Alchemy Workshop

Alchemy Workshop
There are 2 Alchemy Workshops, located near the entrance areas of 2 alliances. Capturing them grants substantial points continuously, and they should be prioritized at the start of the battle.


### Healing Tent

Healing Tent
There are 4 pre-built Healing Tents on the battlefield. Capturing them not only grants a moderate amount of points but also heals wounded soldiers.

[Healing Tent] heals 15 Wounded Soldiers every 10s.


### Strategic Outpost I

Strategic Base I
There are numerous strategic facilities on the battlefield. Capturing them provides a small amount of points over time and various benefits.

[Migration Portal] City relocation cooldown reduced by 50%.
[Observatory] Allied point efficiency increased by 10%.


### Strategic Outpost II

Strategic Base II
After 10 minutes of battle, all remaining strategic bases will be accessible. Capturing them grants a small amount of points over time and combat buffs.

[War Relic] Increases allied Heroes\' ATK, DEF, and HP by 15%.
[Cursed Altar] Reduces enemy Heroes\' ATK, DEF, and HP by 15%.


### Occupation Points

[b]Occupation Points[/b]
Upon occupation, points will be generated continuously.
Occupied buildings will store points that can be plundered after a period of time.


### Breaching Points

[b]Breach Points[/b]
When a building is breached, Medkits will scatter around the area, and both sides can dispatch squads to collect them, earning a small amount of points.


### Camp Points

[b]Camp Points[/b]
At the 13-minute mark of the battle, camps storing a large amount of herbs will appear. Deploy troops to gather them and you earn a small amount of points over time.


### Hunt

Defeating Blights in the world grants resources and items. Powerful Elite Blights require an alliance to launch an attack. The higher the Blight\'s level, the greater the rewards for defeating it.


### Falcon Tower

Completing various quests in the Falcon Tower grants generous rewards.


### Gather

Dispatch troops to occupy Resource Spots for resource gathering. The higher the level of the Resource Spots, the more resources collected.


### Battle

Engage in battles with Doctors from different alliances in the world, such as plundering territories or attacking resource points. Battles may deplete your soldiers, so proceed with caution!


### Basic Rules

Upon acquisition, you receive an extra reward each time you complete a Falcon Quest, with a chance to obtain UR items from the reward chest.


### Promote

Raising stars boosts abilities, improving the quality and quantity of chests obtained from quests.


### Declare War

R4 or R5 can declare war on adjacent cities, allowing alliance members to siege.


### Siege

Attack cities, defeat garrisons, and deplete durability to earn participation rewards.


### Ranking

After the event, ranking rewards are settled based on the level and number of occupied cities.


### Declare War

R4 or R5 can declare war on adjacent cities, allowing alliance members to siege.


### Defeat the Garrison

During a siege, defeat the city\'s garrison first. The garrison is strong, so proceed with caution.


### Reduce Durability

After defeating the garrison, deplete the city\'s durability. The more participants, the faster the process.


### Capture City

Once City DEF is depleted, total points from defeating the garrison and reducing durability will be tallied. The alliance with the highest points takes control of the city.


### Reveal

When the countdown for the Covert Operation is less than 10m, it will be visible to everyone. To avoid losses, collect quest rewards promptly!


### Steal

Completed quest points will show a chest bubble. Tap the bubble to steal generous rewards. Note: Each quest can be stolen once per person per day.


### Share

Each quest point can be stolen up to 3 times. Share the quest point with alliance members for the joy.


### Search

In the world thumbnail, view completed quest points, with different colors for quest bubbles of varying quality.


### ATK

During the Royal City battle, Alliance members can attack the city alone or in teams.


### Occupy

Conquer and maintain control of the Royal City to achieve ultimate victory in the Royal City Scramble!


### Settlement

At the end of the Event, claim generous rewards based on earned Honor Points!


### Rules

Royal City Scramble Rules:
1, The Royal City Scramble opens once a week, and only alliances occupying Lv.6 cities are eligible to participate.
2, The Royal City Scramble lasts for 8 hours. The first alliance to occupy for 4 hours, or the one with the longest total occupation time by the end, will be the ultimate victor.
3, The leader of the victorious alliance becomes the King.
4, The Royal City is surrounded by the Cursed Land, and territories relocated there cannot activate shields, while the Falcon Tower ceases operation, halting exploration quests.


### Attack Npc

Two types of Plague Rats lurk in Crystal Cluster Valley. Defeating monsters in the valley grants Skill Crystals, which upgrade Valley Skills and help you dominate the valley!


### Attack/Scout Crystal Cluster

Crystal Cluster Valley contains Lv.1–Lv.3 clusters. Higher levels purify Purified Crystals faster, and the total Purified Crystals decides the final outcome. Unoccupied clusters are guarded. Defeat the defenders before you can harvest them!


### Free Relocate

You can relocate for free once every 360s, then enter cooldown. During cooldown, relocation requires Targeted Relocate items, with the cost increasing by 1 item each time based on the previous use, up to 5 items per relocation.


### Occupy Sacred Crystal Cave

Sacred Cave lies at the center of Crystal Cluster Valley. For the first 23 minutes after the valley opens, Sacred Cave is under protection. Once the protection ends, all players in the valley can march to the center to occupy Sacred Cave, but each player can dispatch only one team to contest it. Based on occupation ranking, the top 10 players receive a large amount of Purified Crystals as rewards, which is directly added to their total gathering amount during settlement!


### Instant Crystal Cluster

Instant Crystal is a special Crystal Cluster in the valley that players can harvest quickly. It appears in six waves during the valley event at 10, 11, 12, 20, 21, and 22 minutes. It is a fast way to gain Purified Crystals during the event.


### Crystal Skill

You can obtain Skill Crystals by defeating Plague Rats in the valley. After collecting enough Skill Crystals, you can learn Valley Skills. Each level has two skills, but you can only choose one. The Lv.1-Lv.4 skills are passive skills that take effect automatically after learning, while the Lv.5 skill is an active skill that you must cast manually!


### Main city cannot be attacked

In the valley, players cannot launch attacks or scout other players\' main cities. Feel free to attack or gather resource points!


### Specialty Choice

Specialty Selection:
Handyman: All development relies on technological progress. Craftsmen earn respect through exceptional talent, becoming indispensable pillars of their cities.
Legion Commander: Before absolute power, all other voices fade to insignificance. Formidable commanders sweep away all obstacles and conquer all directions.


### Era Skill

Era Skills: Activate Era Skills by upgrading your Specialty Level. Each Era Skill unlocks unique functions, accelerating your Sanctuary development while enhancing your personal capabilities.


### Specialty Skill

Specialty Skills: Each Specialty possesses unique Specialty Skills. Gaining a Specialty Level grants Specialty Skill Points. Upon reaching the designated Specialty Level, Lords can manually use Specialty Skill Points to activate Specialty Skills. (Some Specialty Skills are Active Skills, requiring activation within the World or Main City)


### Specialty Level

Lords gain Specialty Exp by defeating monsters in the World. Accumulate Specialty EXP to increase Specialty Level. Each Specialty Level gain grants Specialty Skill Points. Upon reaching the designated Specialty Level, Lords can manually use Specialty Skill Points to activate Specialty Skills.


### Specialty Reset

Use a Specialty Reset Book to reset your current Specialty and select a different one. Upon reset, all Specialty Skills are cleared and must be relearned.


### EXP Overflow

Once your Specialty Level reaches its Cap, you\'ll continue earning Specialty EXP at the end of the era, excess Specialty EXP converts to Items at a 30% rate. Converted Items are sent via Mail and can be used to upgrade your Specialty Level in the next era.


### RES Level

RES Level is crucial for battling era Monsters. Upgrading RES Level grants RES attributes, helping you overcome era Monsters with ease. When an era ends, RES Level becomes inactive, and its RES attributes are lost. Upon the next era\'s start, they reactivate.


### RES Attribute

RES is the sole attribute for battling era Monsters. Each Monster has its own RES attribute. When your RES exceeds the Monster\'s RES, you\'ll defeat them easily; if your RES falls short, you\'ll face disadvantages or even defeat in battle.
