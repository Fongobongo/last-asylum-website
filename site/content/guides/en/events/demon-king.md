The **Demon King (Blight Ranger)** is a recurring world boss event on the global map in **Last Asylum: Plague**, allowing commanders to assault a massive infected entity for premium rewards: diamonds, equipment chests, hero recruitment tickets, and resources.

---

## 📅 Schedule & Participation Requirements {#schedule}

* **Event Days:** The Demon King spawns on the world map **every Tuesday and Friday** (starting immediately after the server reset at 00:00 server time / 02:00 UTC).
* **Sanctuary Requirement:** Available to all commanders with **Sanctuary Level 8 or higher**.
* **Daily Attack Cap:** Each commander has **up to 10 attacks per day**.
* **Zero Troop Loss:** Battles against the Demon King do not result in permanent soldier deaths — wounded soldiers are routed to the Infirmary with zero permanent CP reduction.

---

## 🧙 Class Vulnerability: Deploy Mage / Warlock Heroes! {#mages-weakness}

> [!TIP] Core Counter Mechanic: Demon King Takes Extra Damage from Mages!
> The Blight Ranger is inherently **vulnerable to heroes of the Mage / Warlock class**. Mages deal significantly amplified damage throughout the fight.

To maximize your damage ranking and secure top milestone chests:
1. **Build a Dedicated Mage Lineup:** Prioritize high single-target DPS spellcasters such as **Marlena**, **Celia**, **Evelyn**, and **Catherine**.
2. **Faction Resonance:** Deploy 5 heroes belonging to the same caster faction to trigger the maximum **+25% ATK & HP synergy bonus**.
3. **Single-Target Focus:** Equip and skill heroes with heavy single-target nuke abilities over area-of-effect (AoE) utility skills.

---

## ⚡ The March Trick: 30-Second Fixed Transit Recall {#march-trick}

> [!IMPORTANT] Secret Mechanic: Demon King marches always take exactly 30 seconds!
> Regardless of the distance between your Sanctuary and the Demon King anywhere on the global map, **both the outbound march and the return march are strictly locked to exactly 30 seconds**.

### Tactical Applications:
* **"Shortcutting" Long March Returns:** If your troops are stuck returning from a distant resource tile, alliance outpost, or long-distance expedition (5–10 minutes away), retarget that march directly onto the Demon King. The transit timer instantly resets to 30 seconds, bringing your legions home half a minute after impact!
* **Emergency Defense Recall:** If an incoming hostile rally is marching toward your base while your main force is far afield, redirecting an attack at the Demon King pulls your forces back to your walls in 30 seconds flat.
* **Save March Speedups:** There is no need to expend expensive boots or march boosts — the boss mechanic handles rapid transit for free.

## 🎁 Event Rewards {#rewards}

1. **Per-Attack Milestone Chests:** Each strike awards equipment essence chests, Antitoxin, diamonds, and resource bundles.
2. **Daily Damage Leaderboard:** High-ranking commanders receive universal UR Omni Shards and Golden Recruit Tickets delivered via in-game mail.
3. **Alliance Progression:** All damage dealt by guild members pools toward guild milestone reward boxes.

---

## 📋 Tuesday & Friday Checklist {#checklist}

1. Locate the Demon King on the world map upon reset (Tuesday & Friday).
2. Switch Squad 1 to your dedicated **Mage / Warlock heavy roster**.
3. Complete all **10 daily attacks** before the daily reset.
4. Utilize the **30-second march recall trick** whenever field squads need rapid transit home.
