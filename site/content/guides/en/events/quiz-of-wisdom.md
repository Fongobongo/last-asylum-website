**Quiz of Wisdom** is a recurring community trivia event in **Last Asylum: Plague** where commanders are tested on their knowledge of game mechanics, lore, building prerequisites, faction counters, and hero skills.

Answering all 15 questions correctly awards maximum tier milestone chests packed with free diamonds, recruit tickets, speedups, and precious progression materials.

Use our interactive search tool below! Simply type a keyword from the question (e.g. `sanctuary`, `raven`, `celia`, `15`, `rat king`), and the verified community answer will appear instantly.

## Key Question Categories Overview

* **General Mechanics:** Plunder limits (Caravans: 4/day, Covert Ops: 5/day), stamina claim interval (4 hours), and explorer camp progression.
* **Buildings & Gates:** Raven Gear unlock level (**Sanctuary 15**), antitoxin production (Antitoxin Workshop), and training ground mechanics.
* **Factions & Counters:** The rock-paper-scissors counter triangle:
  * **Warrior counters Warlock**
  * **Warlock counters Ranger**
  * **Ranger counters Warrior**
  * Deploying 5 heroes of the same faction grants maximum squad stat amplification (+25% ATK & HP).
* **VIP Milestones:** Chat VIP badge (**VIP 3**), Quick Expedition (**VIP 8**), Auto Covert Dispatch (**VIP 10**), and Super Mode (**VIP 12**).
* **Game Lore:** The Rat King injured Celia, William was the first rescued patient, and Plague Doctor masks are filled with aromatic spices and herbs.
