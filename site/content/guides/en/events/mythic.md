Mythic Treasure is the big pirate-themed event, and it is built like a funnel: seven tabs, six of them angled toward spending, and a handful of genuinely free value buried inside. The F2P play is to know exactly which tabs pay and which exist to tempt you — then grind the free ones daily.

This guide walks all seven tabs with the corrected market priority, the fish economy, the Buried Treasure strategy, and the anchor-coin expiry rule that catches everyone.

## The Seven Tabs at a Glance {#the-7-tabs}

| Tab | What it is | F2P verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pirate's Mythic Treasure | Battle pass | Free track only |
| 2. Pirate's Game | Slot machine | Free draws only, never chase |
| 3. Pirate Market | Anchor Coin shop | Buy UR fragments daily |
| 4. Grand Feast | Server-wide fish event | One of the best F2P sections — do it |
| 5. Delicious Hunt | Sea Fish farming | Farm to the 300 daily cap |
| 6. Deep Sea's Gift | Money top-up rewards | Skip as F2P |
| 7. Buried Treasure | Mini-game | 5 free runs + the suicide play |

## Tab 1: Pirate's Mythic Treasure (Battle Pass) {#tab-1-pass}

The headline battle pass. The **advanced track costs 499** and pays in **Anchor Coins, Pirate Gold and exclusive chests**. If you spend on one thing in this event, this is the candidate — but the free track still pays, and everything below works without the pass.

## Tab 2: Pirate's Game (Slot Machine) {#tab-2-slots}

- Paid spins award **Anchor Coins**, and there is a **5X draw** option.
- It is **pure RNG — do not chase it.** The slot machine is the single worst value target in the event, and it is designed to feel almost due.
- Take the **free draws** every day and walk away. That is the entire tab.

## Tab 3: Pirate Market — Corrected Priority {#tab-3-market}

The market takes Anchor Coins, and the buy order matters more than anywhere else in the event:

1. **UR fragments — BUY EVERY DAY.** Top priority, no debate. This is the guaranteed hero value the event offers.
2. **Curio Chest daily** — if you have spare anchors after the fragments.
3. **Final day leftovers go to Raven Essence or speedups** — spend the remainder before the exchange closes (see [the expiry rule](#anchor-expiry)).

And the two hard DON'Ts:

- **Do not buy speedups or resources with anchors.** They are available everywhere else in the game; UR fragments are not.
- **Pearls only raise hero HP — "not super useful."** Skip.

One more trap to know about: the **4,000-anchor ship skin exists**. It is pretty. **Do not sacrifice guaranteed rewards for it** — four thousand anchors is the entire market's fragment income for the event.

## Tab 4: Grand Feast {#tab-4-grand-feast}

The server-wide banquet: submit **10 Sea Fish for 1 Banquet EXP**, with **top-50 rewards** for the biggest contributors. The community verdict is blunt — **"one of the best F2P sections"** of the event. It costs nothing but the fish you farm anyway (see [Delicious Hunt](#tab-5-fish)), and it pays server-visible rewards. Submit daily, every day, without exception.

## Tab 5: Delicious Hunt (Sea Fish Farming) {#tab-5-fish}

Sea Fish come from your normal daily targets:

- Falcon Tower
- Resource Monsters
- Elite Blight
- Black Ops
- Wandering Blight

The critical number: **DAILY CAP 300 fish**. Farm past it and you are killing mobs for nothing — the counter stops. Hit 300, stop, bank the time. The fish feed the Grand Feast, which is why the two tabs together are the F2P core of the whole event.

## Tab 6: Deep Sea's Gift {#tab-6-deep-sea}

One clarification that saves diamonds: this tab rewards **money top-ups, NOT diamond spending**. Unlike a diamond-spend milestone event, there is nothing a F2P account can do here. Skip the tab entirely — it is not aimed at you.

## Tab 7: Buried Treasure (The F2P Mini-Game) {#tab-7-buried}

The match mini-game, and the most misunderstood tab:

- **5 free attempts per day.**
- Free skills: **Remove, Recall, Refresh.**
- Rewards are **NOT for clearing the stage** — they are granted **ONE BY ONE AS MATCHED**.
- **Collected rewards are SAFE even if you fail the run.** Matched = banked.

Which produces the counterintuitive F2P strategy, the **"suicide for rewards"** play: grab the board's rewards, then **intentionally lose**. Clearing is not the goal — matching rewards off the board is. Failing costs nothing and keeps the matched loot.

The one caveat: **stage 2 is near-impossible without spending** — it wants roughly **26 clears**. Do not burn attempts or money chasing it; farm stage 1's free value instead.

## CRITICAL: Anchor Coins Do Not Carry Over {#anchor-expiry}

> Anchor Coins DO NOT carry over — unused coins convert to speedups when the exchange closes. Really use them all before then.

Speedups are the consolation prize, not the goal (see the [market priority](#tab-3-market) — anchors buy UR fragments, speedups buy speedups). Ending the event with anchors left is straight value loss: everything they could have bought in the market was strictly better than the conversion rate.

## The F2P Daily Checklist {#f2p-checklist}

1. Farm **300 Sea Fish** ( Delicious Hunt ) and stop at the cap.
2. Submit fish to the **Grand Feast**.
3. Play **5 Buried Treasure attempts** — match rewards, suicide, repeat.
4. Take the **free Pirate's Game draws**.
5. Spend anchors on the guaranteed value — **UR fragments first**.

## Community Verdict {#community-verdict}

Mythic Treasure is **heavily P2W-flavored** — the pass, the slots and the top-up tab are all built for spenders. But the F2P sections — **Grand Feast, Buried Treasure, and the fish farming** — are worth doing every single day of the event. Grind those, ignore the rest, and leave nothing on the table when the exchange closes.
