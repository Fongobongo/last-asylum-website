Thief Hunt is the **alliance stamina event**: you spend stamina attacking thieves on the map, and thief kills roll for a chance to spawn a **Thief Leader boss** the whole alliance can rally. It converts your spare stamina into the exchange shop — one of the best value shops in the game if you buy it in the right order.

This guide covers boss spawn mechanics, the technique for spawning high-level bosses, the exchange priority list, and the boss squad.

## How It Works {#how-it-works}

- Attack thieves on the map with stamina. Kills spawn the Thief Leader boss for an alliance rally — **the spawn is rolled per thief killed**, not per event day or per player.
- Bosses spawn **on the first kill or after 20** — the roll happens early or after a streak. One alliance reports **always 8 or more**; expect variance and keep killing.
- Points and rewards **do not increase later in the event**. There is zero reason to hoard stamina — **spend it immediately** to roll for the big bosses sooner.

## Boss Levels by Server {#boss-levels}

| Server age | Common boss levels |
|---|---|
| Typical | 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30, rarely 35 |
| Older servers | 40 / 65 / 85 / 90 |

- Your boss level **range is random per player and does not change** — you roll your range and you keep it. It is **not tied to your might**, so a low-might player can roll high-level bosses.
- **Post-July-08 patch: spawns are accurate to server growth.** Since that patch, boss levels track how developed the server is — no more level 10 bosses on a year-old server.

## The Nonstop-Kill Technique for Level 30/35 {#spawn-technique}

The verified technique for pushing boss levels to 30 and 35:

> Kill thieves one by one WITHOUT letting your troops return to base — nonstop, back-to-back marches with zero idle time.

Marches returning to base break the streak; consecutive kills roll the spawn repeatedly. Chain your targets so the next march leaves the moment the last one lands, and the high-level spawns come far more often.

## The Exchange Priority List {#exchange}

The exchange shop is the real reward, and the community consensus order is stable:

### Week 1 Only {#week-1}

1. **Scrolls** — 4 for Alliance Duel tech, **then never again**. Duel tech wants exactly four; after that they are dead weight.

### Always, Every Event {#always}

1. **Raven Essence** — every event, no exceptions. The Raven system is permanently bottlenecked on it (see the [Raven guide](/raven/)).

### High Priority {#high-priority}

1. Stamina
2. Speedups
3. Raven chest
4. Epigraphs *when available*

### Late Game {#late-game}

1. **Herbs** — never enough of them; late-game accounts buy herbs every event.

### Alternative Priority List {#alt-list}

1. Stamina
2. LV3 Raven chest
3. Blue/purple epigraph chests
4. Research/construction speedups
5. Recruitment tickets

And one inventory rule: **the scroll limit carries over event to event**. If you hit the scroll cap, that cap follows you into the next event — another reason the "four then never again" rule exists.

## The Boss Rally Squad {#boss-squad}

The community boss comp, built for damage over time:

| Row | Heroes | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Front | Arthur + Bella | Survive longer — every extra second of survival stacks more damage |

The logic: **longer survival = more damage stacked**. A boss fight is not a burst check — the squad that stands the longest deals the most, so prioritize survival over raw attack. Full hero details are in the [Heroes guide](/heroes/).
