The **Dwarven Mine** is a recurring 7-day mining event: you spend **Pickaxes** to dig tiles, find jackpots to climb floors, and the player who clears **100 floors** within the event takes the top prize — an **MR gear blueprint** (a max-tier gear piece). The entire event is decided before it starts: whoever hoarded pickaxes wins, whoever starts empty pays real money to catch up.

This guide covers pickaxe income, the F2P hoarding target, reward choice strategy, and where the money pit is.

## Mechanics {#mechanics}

- **7-day duration**, recurring event.
- You dig a grid of tiles with pickaxes; finding the **jackpot tile** levels you up to the next floor with better rewards.
- **Floors keep rising with each jackpot** — 100 floors total.
- **Top prize at Floor 100: an MR gear blueprint.**
- The wheel attached to the event can also roll **red (max-rarity) gear** — the true endgame equipment tier.

## The 750-Pickaxe Hoarding Rule {#hoarding}

> F2P reality check: the event is not "played" — it is **pre-funded**. Save at least **750 pickaxes** across event cycles before you start digging seriously.

With fewer than ~750 pickaxes you have no realistic path to Floor 100's MR blueprint. Spending pickaxes off-cycle just burns the resource you needed next time.

### Pickaxe Sources (Free) {#sources}

- Daily and event **quests**
- **Training troops**
- **Falcon Tower quests**
- **Alliance Island donations** (up to 6×)

Paid packs exist but are aggressively priced — assembling MR gloves through packs runs around **$500**. If you're not a spender, just hoard.

## Reward Choice: Herbs vs Gearstones {#reward-choice}

Each climb lets you pick between two reward lines:

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Herbs** (~8M per pick, with luck variance) | ✅ **Best for anyone approaching endgame.** Herbs gate high-tier research — the permanent bottleneck. 100 floors × multi-M herbs is a game-changer. |
| **Gearstones** | ❌ Once you're deep in the game you'll sit on hundreds of thousands of unspendable stones. The bottleneck is blueprints, not stones. |

## The Flow {#flow}

1. Between events: **hoard pickaxes**, do not dig.
2. Event opens: commit the full stockpile in one push.
3. Dig → find jackpot → floor rises → repeat, choosing **Herbs** at every reward fork.
4. Floor 100 lands the MR blueprint; the wheel shots at red gear are bonus along the way.
