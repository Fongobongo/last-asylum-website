The **Final Dawn** event is the definitive 7-day developmental milestone of your early journey in Last Asylum: Plague. Its primary focus is accelerating your Sanctuary Might and steadily unlocking tiered milestone rewards: universal speedups, massive resource crates, hero fragments, and essential gear.

Formulated by veteran commanders (oψsvge英俊的, Server K250), this guide provides a phase-by-phase tactical blueprint for the entire 7-day timeline.

---

## 📅 4 Tactical Phases of the 7-Day Plan {#phases}

### Phase 1: Days 1–2 — Foundations
- **Core Focus:** Rapidly advancing your Sanctuary (Base) level.
- Upgrade only buildings strictly required as prerequisites for the next Sanctuary tier.
- Keep Research running continuously 24/7 without a single second of idle queue time.
- Recruit Survivors and assign them immediately to key production facilities.
- **Cardinal Rule:** Never allow construction or research builders to sit idle.

### Phase 2: Days 3–4 — Power Push
- **Core Focus:** Escalating hero combat power, skill tiers, and military strength.
- Funnel Antitoxin into your primary carry and frontline tank (Arthur, Claire, Marlena).
- Upgrade hero skill levels and equip starter gear sets.
- Target high-yield Might buildings: Sanctuary, Barracks, and Raven Tower.
- Actively participate in Alliance assistance taps to shave hours off building timers.

### Phase 3: Days 5–6 — Major Push
- **Core Focus:** Deploying stockpiled speedups and resource caches.
- Systematically spend accumulated building and universal speedups on long-duration projects.
- Queue expensive, high-Might technologies in the Research Laboratory.
- Train and **promote** soldiers: promoting lower-tier troops (T1→T2→T3) generates milestone points faster and more efficiently than training raw recruits.
- Open bagged resources strictly on-demand to satisfy upgrade shortfalls.

### Phase 4: Day 7 — FINAL PUSH 🔥
- **Core Focus:** Milestone auditing and sweeping all remaining rewards.
- Audit the Final Dawn event dashboard to identify unreached milestone thresholds.
- Liquidate remaining speedups, Hero EXP, Raven materials, and resource reserves.
- Complete the specific upgrades delivering the highest Might-per-minute value.
- **IMPORTANT:** Claim every single Final Dawn milestone chest before the server reset (00:00 Server Time / 02:00 UTC)!

## 🏆 The Golden Priority Chain {#priorities}

When allocating builders, speedups, and materials, adhere strictly to this sequence:
$$\text{Sanctuary} \longrightarrow \text{Prerequisite Buildings} \longrightarrow \text{Research} \longrightarrow \text{Heroes} \longrightarrow \text{Troops} \longrightarrow \text{Resources} \longrightarrow \text{Alliance}$$

1. **Sanctuary:** Drives account caps and awards the largest single Might jumps.
2. **Prerequisites:** Wall, Laboratory, and Military Barracks.
3. **Research:** Permanent, non-lootable technological power.
4. **Heroes:** Level, star rank, and active skill breakpoints for your first squad.
5. **Troops:** Sustained production of your highest unlocked tier.

---

## ⚠️ The Golden Strategic Secret {#strategy-secret}

> **Never exhaust your speedups and resource bags on Day 1!**

The most common pitfall is blowing all starter speedups within the first 24 hours. When Days 5–7 arrive with massive high-tier reward milestones, unprepared players find their bags empty and stall out.

**The winning approach:** Accumulate speedups and resource crates through Days 1–4 using natural build timers and alliance assists, then execute a coordinated, explosive push during Days 5–7.
