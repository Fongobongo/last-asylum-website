Guild Wagon and Caravans are the game's daily logistics events — and both are **zero-risk, zero-stamina** income that too many players skip. The wagon is a 10-minute coordination exercise; the caravans are a permanent UR-shard farm. Neither costs troops or real losses, so the community rule is simple: **ALWAYS run them.**

This guide covers the wagon's roles and chest rules, the Golden Wagon, the caravan strategy, and cross-server plunder with the box priority.

## Guild Wagon: The Free Daily Run {#guild-wagon-basics}

- **1 free wagon per alliance per day**, resetting around **11 AM KST**.
- When the payment screen appears, **the free one has been used** — that is the tell that the daily free run is gone for the whole alliance.
- **Paid extra runs exist — almost nobody buys them.** Ignore the paywall.
- **Guild Gift levels raise the wagon level** — the shop gifts that raise gift level also upgrade what the wagon drops.
- **Lv. 25 Alliance Gift Privilege (September 2026):** Reaching Alliance Gift Lv. 25 permanently grants an extra perk: **each Regular Wagon of the Alliance Trade Caravan carries 1 additional passenger**, significantly boosting passive daily rewards for members.

## Wagon Master, Guards, and VIPs {#wagon-master}

The roles:

- An **online R4/R5 appoints an online member as Wagon Master**.
- The Master **collects tickets from members** (some auto-convert) for pool refreshes — **5 refreshes make a "Golden Wagon."**
- **Guards AND VIPs both claim 2 chests.** VIPs just ride — being a VIP is not a downgrade, the chests are identical.
- **Low CP? Pick strong Guards to protect the wagon.** The run's safety scales with guard strength.
- **Guards must be online.** Coordinate **before** starting the run, or risk an undefended plunder — some guilds deliberately delay the Master appointment to run the wagon at night when their guards are on.

## The Chest Rules (The Common Mistake) {#chest-rules}

> The 2 chests are for the GUARDS, not the Master. The most common wagon mistake in the game.

The full reward distribution:

| Role | Reward |
|---|---|
| Guards | 2 chests each |
| VIPs | 2 chests each (identical to guards — they just ride) |
| Passengers | 5 random draws + 1 vehicle chest each |
| Wagon Master | ALL items from all 4 wagons |

Read that last row twice — **the Master receives ALL items from all 4 wagons.** The appointment is a real payout, which is exactly why the chest confusion persists: the guards' 2 chests come on top of a share, not out of the Master's pool.

## ⏱️ Caravan Phases, Plunder Limits & Gift Perks {#phases-and-limits}

1. **Waiting Phase (4 Hours):**
   - Before departure, an alliance caravan enters a **4-hour staging period**.
   - **Wagon Master:** During this window, the Master can refresh carriage rewards. After **5 refreshes**, the caravan is guaranteed to upgrade into a **Golden Caravan**. The Master also assigns guards.
   - **Passengers:** Members can select and switch carriages freely. **At 5 minutes before departure**, 5 random queued passengers are locked in.

2. **Transit Phase & Plunder Rules:**
   - Outside players and foreign kingdoms can attack the caravan en route.
   - **Each carriage can be pillaged up to 3 times** (looters can steal up to 3 cargo slots).
   - High-power guards prevent loss and ensure 100% payout to all participants!

3. **Alliance Gift Level Milestones:**
   - When the Alliance Gift reaches **Lv. 8, Lv. 12, Lv. 16, Lv. 20, and Lv. 25**, the daily free caravan gains permanent capacity expansions and reward multipliers.
   - Purchasing a Golden Caravan instantly unlocks all max-tier privileges without delay.

## Caravans: The UR Shards Strategy {#caravans}

Caravans are the daily caravan event running alongside the wagon:

- Core decisions: **which caravans to attack, how to set up your alliance caravan, and maximizing daily rewards.**
- **Caravans and Covert Ops never count toward the plunder limit** — they are exempt from the daily cap that governs normal raiding (see the [Economy guide](/economy/)).
- **No real troop loss, no stamina cost.** Zero risk, every day.
- **ALWAYS run them.** And remember from the [KvK](/events/kvk/) guide: **caravans and Covert Ops score for both KvK and Alliance Duel** — the same run pays twice.

### Korpez' daily caravan routine

- **Failed attacks DO NOT consume an attempt!** The most critical takeaway for newcomers: if your attack on an enemy caravan fails, **your 4 daily plunder attempts remain 100% intact**. You can safely test defender strength without fear of wasting a daily run.
- **Attack only caravans carrying 2+ UR shards visible in the loot preview.** A 2-shard carrier often drops 1 per hit; 3–4-shard targets are rarer and worth two hits. Skip everything that doesn't show UR shards.
- **Send your own caravans at off-peak hours and strictly 1 at a time.** When rival kingdoms are asleep, routes are completely safe. Stagger your dispatches one by one, always escorting with your primary **Squad 1**.
- **Save UR-tier caravan refreshes for Alliance Duel days** — only then does the quest checkbox matter; on standard days stick with purple or natural gold caravans.
- **Share plunder reports in alliance chat** — caravan reports are shareable directly from the Records tab, so allies can jump the same juicy targets.

## Plundering Other Servers {#plundering-servers}

The cross-server caravan route:

1. Open **Caravan Trade** and check the green checkmark on **"Exclude this server's caravans"** — it filters out home server caravans and reveals foreign ones. Cross-server dispatch can also be launched directly while viewing status.
2. Go to **Other Caravans**, tap a caravan — and the **Plunder button teleports you to that Kingdom**.
3. Close the popup — and you can **freely roam that Kingdom** while you are there.

## Box Priority: Gold, Purple, Blue {#box-priority}

When you are staring at a foreign map full of caravans, the target order is:

| Box color | Value |
|---|---|
| GOLD | Highest — UR tier. Always the target. |
| Purple | Very good — SSR tier. Priority target when gold is out of reach. |
| Blue | Backup — SR tier. Filler when the map is picked clean. |

- **Check the star rating and rewards before attacking** — the preview shows what the box pays.
- **Better color + higher stars = better rewards.** Color first, stars as the tiebreaker.
