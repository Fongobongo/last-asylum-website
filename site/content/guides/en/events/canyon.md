Canyon Conquest is the **alliance lane battle**: three lanes, and the alliance that wins **2 of 3 lanes** takes the match. But the format is a **relay** — the weakest players fight first, and a player **stays in the lane until they lose**. Understanding what that relay does to lineup strategy is the whole event.

This guide covers the relay rules, the reward structure, the lane-priority strategy with its main variants, the known bugs, and why this is the safest event in the game.

## The Relay Rules {#relay-rules}

1. **Weakest fight first.** Each lane starts with its lowest-CP players and escalates upward only when someone loses.
2. **The player stays until they lose, then the next enters.** A winning player holds the lane and keeps fighting the next challenger — there is no rotation after a win.
3. **Max 3 fights per player.** After three fights you are done for the match.
4. **15 players per lane.** The alliance splits its roster across the three lanes.

Consequence of the relay: **if your side wins every fight at 2 kills each, only 8 of 15 lane players ever fight.** Seven players sit the entire match without spending a fight. That fact drives every lineup decision below.

## Rewards and the 3-Kill Tier {#rewards}

- The **3-kill reward is tier-gated** — you need all three of your fights to be kills to hit the top personal tier.
- Because stronger players deep in the lane face stronger opponents, the 3-kill tier usually belongs to the top of the roster — plan your personal expectations accordingly.

## Rounds and Groups {#rounds-and-groups}

- **After every 5 rounds the match ends and restarts from Registration.** Re-register each cycle.
- **Win and you move to an upper group; lose and you stay in the same or drop to a weaker group.** The ladder self-sorts alliances over time.
- A **"bye" is possible even at a full 15** — an odd number of registered lanes means someone advances without fighting. It is luck of the draw, not a bug.

## Known Bugs {#known-bugs}

- **Replaced after 2 wins without losing** — the relay should keep you in after wins, but some players get pulled after two victories. Report it if it hits you.
- **Registered players excluded from round 2.** Stale registrations can strand you out of the next round. Removal from the list is **R4/R5 only** — if you are stuck, ask leadership to clear you so you can re-register.

## Lane Priority Strategy {#lane-strategy}

> You don't need to win EVERY fight — you need the RIGHT lanes.

### The Consensus Setup {#consensus-setup}

1. **Prioritize 2 of the 3 lanes.** Winning the match needs 2 lanes, not 3 — pouring roster evenly into all three just guarantees losing the third anyway.
2. **Split the top-30 evenly into two strong lanes**, and fill the remaining bodies into the third.

### The Refinement: Two Strongest to the Weak Lane {#weak-lane-refinement}

1. Send your **2 strongest players to the WEAK third lane** — they carry it.
2. The logic is relay arithmetic: your strongest players parked in the strong lanes **often never fight**, because the lane's weaker players win out before the aces are ever needed. In the weak lane, they are the difference between 0-1 and a live lane.

### The Counterpoint That Worked {#counterpoint}

- **3 balanced teams with the top-3 player in each** has also verifiably worked — an anchor per lane instead of a conceded lane.

Neither setup is wrong in a vacuum. The correct read: **check who won and lost where in the next round.** Your opponents change group by group, and the lane setup that won this match may need rebalancing next round. Adapt, don't autopilot.

## Why This Is the Best Zero-Risk Event {#why-zero-risk}

Canyon Conquest has **no troop loss, no resource risk, and no real stamina cost** — unlike [KvK](/events/kvk/), where an unshielded city burns. You fight with borrowed tournament power, your real army stays home intact. That makes it one of the **best events for safe rewards** in the game: show up, register, fight your three fights, collect.

The only real cost is lineup attention — and the relay rules above are the entire lineup meta.
