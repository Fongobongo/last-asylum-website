Survival Battle is the **weekly mission event**, and for free-to-play accounts it is the single most important recurring income source in the game: **F2P players can earn roughly 60,000 Skill Badges per week** from it. Skill Badges are the permanent hero-skill bottleneck — this event is how you keep them flowing without spending.

This guide covers how the missions work, the cheap completion tricks, and the one strategic conflict you have to plan around.

## How It Works {#how-it-works}

- Survival Battle is a **weekly event** with a rotating mission list.
- **Mission lists VARY BY SERVER** — your list may not match another server's, so treat every mission guide (including specific mission names) as approximate and check your own in-game list first.
- The payout target for a F2P account is **~60,000 Skill Badges per week** — achievable from the mission rewards when the list is completed efficiently.

Task categories you will see on the lists:

- Technology research missions
- Enhance Raven missions
- Train Soldier missions (see [the barracks trick](#barracks-trick))
- Monster-killing missions (see [stamina missions](#stamina-missions))
- Recruit missions (see [the ticket trick](#recruit-missions))

## The Low-Tier Barracks Trick {#barracks-trick}

Train Soldiers missions do not care about tier — they count heads. So:

1. Queue **cheap, low-tier troops** in your barracks.
2. Each finished batch ticks the Train Soldiers mission at a fraction of the food and speedup cost of real army production.
3. Cancel-proof the timing: low-tier batches finish fast, so you can chain multiple completions inside one event window.

This is the same bodycount logic that powers [Alliance Duel](/events/alliance-duel/) Phase 5 — except there you want high tiers for the big points, while here only the count matters. Train the cheapest troops you have and clear the mission.

## Monster Missions: Save Raven Fruits, Spend Stamina {#stamina-missions}

- **Save your Raven Fruits** for monster-killing missions — do not burn them on ordinary days when nothing is paying for the kill.
- **Spend stamina** to hit monster missions. Stamina spent outside events is the classic F2P waste — the same fights earn event progress when timed to a monster mission.

## The 30-Ticket Recruit Mission {#recruit-missions}

The known cheap completion: one recruit mission clears with **30 recruitment tickets**. That makes it a pure value check — if you have 30 tickets banked, the mission is free; if you do not, weigh it against your [Alliance Duel Phase 4](/events/alliance-duel/) plans, where recruits pay +3,450 points each.

## CRITICAL: The Alliance Duel Overlap {#duel-overlap}

> Some Survival Battle missions overlap with Alliance Duel savings. Decide which to complete now and which to save for the Duel.

This is the one real decision the event forces. Research, recruitment and training missions all want the same resources the duel pays double for:

- **A research mission this week vs Phase 3 scroll-free research on duel Wednesday.**
- **A recruit mission now vs Phase 4's +3,450 per recruit.**
- **A training mission now vs Phase 5's doubled points and +30% speedup value.**

The rule of thumb: compare the Survival Battle reward against the duel points you would sacrifice. Early in a duel cycle, save for the duel; late in the cycle, or when the mission reward is badge-heavy, clear the mission. When the calendars collide, the 60K weekly badge income is the F2P priority — badges have no other farmable source at this scale.

## The Weekly Checklist {#weekly-checklist}

1. Open the event at week start and read **your server's** mission list.
2. Sort missions into "complete now" and "saves for the duel" — before spending anything.
3. Queue low-tier barracks batches for the train missions.
4. Hold Raven Fruits and stamina for the monster missions.
5. Bank 30 recruitment tickets if a recruit mission is live.
6. Collect the full ~60,000 Skill Badges by week's end.

For where those badges go — hero skill priority order — see the [Heroes guide](/heroes/).
