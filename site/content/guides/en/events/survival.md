**Survival Battle** is the premier weekly recurring mission event in Last Asylum: Plague, serving as the financial bedrock for free-to-play commanders: **diligent F2P players consistently pull roughly 60,000 free Skill Badges every single week**.

Skill Badges are the permanent bottleneck across all hero skill trees. This guide breaks down the point engine, the x2 multiplier "Power Hours", and seamless coordination with Alliance Duel phases.

---

## ⚙️ Event Mechanics & Task Categories {#how-it-works}

- Survival Battle runs on a **weekly cadence** featuring rotating objective tracks.
- **CRITICAL NOTE:** Exact task rotations and schedule orders **vary by server**, so always audit your in-game calendar.
- The event revolves around 5 core developmental categories:

| Category | How Points Are Earned |
|---|---|
| **Enhance Raven** | Feeding the Raven Guardian, consuming Raven Fruits, applying Essence, and opening Epigraph chests. |
| **Train Soldiers** | Mobilizing fresh recruits and promoting standing units into higher tiers. |
| **Research Tech** | Finalizing Laboratory technologies, increasing scientific Might, burning speedup minutes. |
| **Build Territory** | Completing structures, elevating Sanctuary level, increasing construction Might. |
| **Enhance Heroes** | Leveling heroes (Antitoxin), upgrading skills (Skill Badges), and star ascension (Omni Shards). |

---

## ⏳ "Power Hours" — The Double-Points Multiplier {#power-hours}

The single biggest edge employed by veteran commanders: **never burn high-value resources during standard hours!**

1. Monitor the **hourglass icon** on the Survival Battle event banner.
2. Throughout each day, specific **4-hour "Power Hour" windows** activate, granting double points and accelerated milestone rewards (**+100% bonus points**).
3. Deploy building, research, and training speedups **strictly during these windows** to max out reward chests with half the material expenditure.

---

## 🛡️ The Low-Tier Barracks Trick {#barracks-trick}

"Train Soldiers" missions count raw headcounts rather than unit tiers:

1. Queue **cheap, low-tier troops (T1–T2)** across all barracks.
2. Low-tier batches consume minimal food and finish in minutes, allowing rapid back-to-back mission triggers.
3. On Friday during Alliance Duel Phase 5, instantly promote these standing low-tier units to your highest unlocked tier, double-dipping points effortlessly!

---

## 🦅 Monster Hunts: Hoard Raven Fruits {#stamina-missions}

- **Conserve Raven Fruits:** Do not burn fruits on off-days when no event points are attached to monster purges.
- **Spend Natural Stamina:** Natural stamina regenerates continuously — bleed it strictly inside active monster elimination windows.

## 🎫 The 30-Ticket Recruitment Milestone {#recruit-missions}

A standout high-efficiency milestone clears for exactly **30 Recruitment Tickets**. If banked, trigger it immediately for an effortless chest sweep. If ticket-constrained, evaluate whether to hold for Thursday's Alliance Duel Phase 4 (+3,450 points per recruit).

## ⚔️ CRITICAL: Alliance Duel Synchronization {#duel-overlap}

> The central F2P tactical balance: managing resource contention between Survival Battle milestones and Alliance Duel phases.

| Resource / Action | Survival Battle | Alliance Duel | Optimal Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Research** | Badge milestone | Phase 3 (Wednesday) | Bank scrolls for Wednesday; clear Survival Battle using scroll-free tech nodes. |
| **Recruit Tickets** | 30-ticket milestone | Phase 4 (Thursday) | Spend exactly 30 tickets if milestone active; funnel remaining tickets to Thursday. |
| **Training Speedups** | Training milestone | Phase 5 (Friday) | Unleash major training speedup reserves on Friday (+100% points, +30% speedup value). |

**Golden Rule:** When calendars conflict, prioritize the weekly **60,000 Skill Badges** milestone floor — there is no equivalent renewable badge farm anywhere in the game!

## 📋 Weekly Survivor Checklist {#weekly-checklist}

1. Audit your server's unique mission rotation every Monday morning.
2. Partition resources between Alliance Duel prerequisites and Survival Battle thresholds.
3. Time all major speedup expenditures to active 4-hour **Power Hour** windows.
4. Exploit the low-tier T1/T2 barracks trick for effortless training points.
5. Sweep all milestone chests to secure your ~60,000 Skill Badges before the weekly reset (00:00 Server Time / 02:00 UTC).
