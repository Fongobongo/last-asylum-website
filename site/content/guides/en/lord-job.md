Lord's Specialty is the player's own development track rather than the heroes', and it grows alongside the city. It runs forty levels, each with its own experience price, and each level pays out a single skill point. Those points end up four times short of what the full skill list costs, so the whole page comes down to spending them well.

## What a level gives

A level itself carries one kind of bonus, hourly production of resistance experience. The bonus grows in a straight line, and the gap between the ends of the scale shows in the table.

| Level | Resistance experience per hour |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1,080 |
| 21 | 22,680 |
| 40 | 43,200 |

Level forty works forty times faster than level one.

## Two branches

The Lord Statue is what opens the Specialty, and until it stands the track is closed. Room for the statue appears at Sanctuary level 10, while both skill branches open only at Sanctuary level 14.

Handyman covers economy and support: instant production, free minutes of building and research, lower resource costs, help for allies on build speed, research speed and clinic capacity, and a shield for oneself or an ally.

Legion Commander covers war: barracks and clinic capacity, training speed, rally damage, reinforcement speed, emergency healing and mines on the map. It also owns Military Supply, which raises the daily plunder limit.

The choice is not final, since both the branch and the point spending can be reset, though only for a dedicated item.

## Forty-four skills, forty points

Skills unlock by lord level, the first right away and then every five levels, with twenty-two of them in each branch. Out of that comes the arithmetic that shapes the whole track. Most skills have five levels, each level costs one point, there are 183 such levels in all, and lord levels hand out forty points, one per level. Less than a quarter can be taken, so the choice weighs more than the grind.

It helps that active abilities are the minority: twenty-eight skills are permanent passive bonuses, eight are instant with a cooldown, four are placed on the map as banners and mines, three are cast on an alliance member, and one grants a shield. Cooldowns on the instant ones run from hours to days.

| Cooldown | Hours |
|---|---|
| Shortest | 2.8 |
| Usual for instants | 23.5 and 47.5 |
| Long | 71.5 and 72 |
| Longest | 95.5 and 119.5 |

Instant abilities save real hours of waiting, but with cooldowns measured in days they are kept for the moments that matter. Passive skills work all the time, and they are what pays points back best.

Four more skills in each branch are tied to levels 45 and 50, which do not exist in the current age: lord levels stop at forty. That is groundwork for the next age.

## Handy vs Legion Commander: which branch to pick {#handy-vs-commander}

Both branches cap at level 40 in the Era of Revival, but they pay off in opposite situations. The short rule from community practice:

- **Handy accelerates the account itself** — construction, research, specialty EXP, production, alliance help.
- **Legion Commander empowers an already-built account in battle** — PvP, raids, KvK, mass fights.

For F2P and most low-spend players **Handy wins**: while Sanctuary timers and T10 research still gate progress, saved days beat a few percent of damage. Commander starts paying once construction and research stop being the bottleneck.

### Handy highlights {#handy-highlights}

- **Alliance contribution skill** — more contribution points for the same donations, spendable in the alliance shop; adds up over a season.
- **Instant production** — a lump sum of resources on demand; most useful mid-development.
- **Daily energy up to +50%** — take it early. More energy means more monsters, more materials, and more specialty EXP, which unlocks the next skills faster.
- **Free research and construction minutes** — around **240 minutes** free at high level, plus **instant timer cuts up to ~1,200 minutes (20 hours)** per use. Best spent on Sanctuary levels, long technologies, T10 requirements, and season research. Total savings over a season run into days.

![Handy energy-boosting skill](/img/dtf/lord-job/02.jpg)

![Faster construction and research in the Handy branch](/img/dtf/lord-job/03.jpg)

- **Combat EXP (up to +30%)** — more specialty EXP per seasonal monster kill. Open it as early as possible so the bonus compounds all season.
- **Help Research / Help Building** — pick an ally and raise research (or construction) speed by **20% for both of you**. Fire before long projects, not short ones.
- **Material Recycle** — up to **−5% research resource cost**; negligible early, serious on late-game techs.
- **Siege War Flag** — a banner placed on the map that buffs allies in its radius, so even an economy build contributes in joint attacks.
- **Alliance Aid** — cuts troop losses by **3%** when reinforcing an ally's territory.
- **Healing Force** — temporarily raises clinic capacity (around **+6%**) for you and a chosen ally; useful before KvK.
- **Bond Shield** — a temporary shield for yourself or an ally (1 hour at level 1, non-stacking, long cooldown). Situational, but can save someone who missed their own shield.

### Legion Commander highlights {#commander-highlights}

![The Legion Commander PvP branch](/img/dtf/lord-job/04.jpg)

- **Fast army recall** — instantly return a march so you can teleport immediately. Genuinely useful in KvK and raids for escaping or repositioning.
- **Instant troops** — a batch of trained soldiers on demand; best after heavy losses.
- **Barracks and clinic capacity** plus **emergency healing** that instantly restores part of the clinic. Strong in mass battles, idle in quiet weeks.
- **Frenzy (up to ~+5% damage)** and a bonus against already-burning sanctuaries — a solid package for organized raids, marginal for players who fight a few battles per KvK.
- **Training speed +3%**, **+20% march speed in the Cursed Lands during Royal Rumble** (dead weight outside that mode), **+6% reinforcement march speed** to allies.
- **Team Raid** — +1% rally damage when you join or lead a rally (non-stacking). Small solo, meaningful for rally leads.
- **Concealed Identity** — hides personal info from enemy battle reports for ~30 minutes. Niche for F2P, interesting for a server's top fighters in KvK.
- **Ally defense pair** — attackers lose ~+1% more troops when you defend an ally's territory, and your reinforcing heroes gain up to ~+5% ATK/DEF/HP.
- **War flag** — up to ~+2% ATK aura for allies in radius; modest alone, noticeable across a packed battle line.
- **Weakened heavies** — extra pressure on the enemy's heavily wounded troops, capped around **1,000 soldiers per day**. Scales with how much PvP you actually fight.

![Training, march, and reinforcement skills of the Legion Commander](/img/dtf/lord-job/05.jpg)

### Verdict by account {#branch-verdict}

- **F2P — Handy.** Your limiter is progression speed: Sanctuary, tech, T10, speedups.
- **Low-spend — Handy.** Purchases shorten timers but late-game research and Sanctuary levels still dominate.
- **Most mid-spend — Handy**, until resources and speedups stop being the constraint.
- **Strong PvP account / whale / alliance war lead — Legion Commander.** Raids, reinforcements, healing, and mass PvP are the daily job, and flags plus support auras multiply the whole formation.

An early T10, a finished key technology, or the next Sanctuary level usually beats a few percent of PvP stats — develop Handy first, and switch to Commander when the fortress no longer holds the account back and the calendar fills with KvK.
