Building in Last Asylum: Plague is a long game — the Sanctuary push from level 1 to level 30 takes months, and the wrong order costs you weeks of it. This page is the community build order: which buildings matter and in what sequence, a level-by-level Sanctuary roadmap, and the exact math of construction speed stacking.

The one question that decides every queue slot: does this make you stronger or faster? If the answer is no, it can wait.

## The Build Priority Order {#priority-order}

When several buildings are available at once, the community order is fixed:

1. **Sanctuary** — always. It gates every other level in the city, the hero level cap, and the troop tiers. Whenever a Sanctuary upgrade is available, it goes into the queue first.
2. **Training Grounds** — troop tiers and training capacity. The army is what clears monsters, wins rallies, and takes plunder home.
3. **Barracks** — soldier capacity. A bigger standing army means more might and more punch per march.
4. **Research Lab** — permanent stat and economy research. Slower to pay off than troops, but it compounds forever.
5. **Infirmary** — heals your wounded after defeats. A small Infirmary means every lost fight bleeds troops you cannot recover.
6. **Wall** — defense. Real until someone stronger arrives anyway, which is why it sits last.

The rule behind the list: if a building does not make you stronger or faster, it is not urgent. Production buildings, depots and decorations still get built — but only when the six above have nothing to do or a gate forces them.

A quick reference for the buildings you will actually be tapping. Translations differ between game clients, so when two names sound alike, check the in-game tooltip for the exact effect on your version.

| Building | What it does | Community notes |
|---|---|---|

| Builder's Hut | Produces free construction speedups. Survivors stationed inside give extra bonuses — swap in better survivors as you recruit them. | Keep at Sanctuary level until Sanctuary 15 for the free speedup stream. |
| Research Lab | Runs your tech tree: economy, military, and development research. | Priority #4 — permanent compounding stats. |
| Barracks / Training Grounds | Names get swapped between translations. One opens troop tiers; the other increases soldier capacity. | Check the in-game tooltip to see which is which in your client, then build the tier-opener first. |
| Infirmary | Heals wounded soldiers after defeats. | Undersized Infirmary = permanent troop losses after every lost fight. |

| Gear / Smelting / Weaving Workshops | Craft and upgrade hero gear from farmed materials. | High Gear Workshop levels arrive with Sanctuary 20 — one more reason 20 matters. |
| Tavern | Hero recruiting — the banner and pull system lives here. | Save diamonds for dated UR events instead of dumping them here. |
| Alliance Hall | Deepens alliance participation and rewards. | Follow the roadmap below — it matters more than new players expect. |

### Barracks vs Training Grounds — the Naming Trap {#barracks-vs-training}

One building opens new troop tiers, and the other increases how many soldiers you can hold. Which name does which depends on the translation of your client — two players arguing about it may both be right. Open the building and read the tooltip: if the listed effect is unlocking tiers, that is your tier-opener and it goes first; if it is soldier capacity, it goes second. Community guides from different regions swap the names freely, so trust the tooltip over the guide title.

## Sanctuary Roadmap by Level {#sanctuary-roadmap}

This is the community roadmap (from the guide by elsiika57) for what to level at each Sanctuary stage. Read it as: while pushing Sanctuary toward level X, bring the listed buildings up to the listed levels. It exists to stop you from leveling buildings you will not need for weeks while the ones you need every day fall behind.

| Sanctuary push | Bring these buildings up |
|---|---|
| Lv 1-10 | Lv 4 Lumber Depot 1, Granary 1, Storage 1. At Sanctuary 5-6: Walls 3 to 5. At Sanctuary 7-8: Training Grounds 4, Alliance Hall 3 to 5. At Sanctuary 9-10: Walls 8-9, Alliance Hall 7, Infirmary 7. |
| Lv 11-18 | At Sanctuary 11: Training Grounds 10, Research Lab 7. At Sanctuary 12-13: Research Lab 11-12, Walls 10, Alliance Hall 11. At Sanctuary 14-15: Research Lab 13-14, Warrior Statue 12, Training Grounds 14. At Sanctuary 16-18: Warrior Statue 14, Research Lab 15-17, Barracks 15, Training Grounds 17. |
| Lv 19-24 | Research Lab 18, Warrior Statue 17, Alliance Hall 18. At Sanctuary 21-22: Research Lab 20, Training Grounds 20, Infirmary 21. At Sanctuary 23-24: Infirmary 22, Research Lab 23, Walls 23. |
| Lv 25-29 | Training Grounds 24, Research Lab 24-25, Warrior Statue 25. At Sanctuary 27-28: Research Lab 26, Training Grounds 26, Barracks 27. At Sanctuary 29: Alliance Hall 28, Research Lab 28. |
| Lv 30 | Sanctuary 30 is the max. Finish every remaining gate. |

Notice the pattern inside the table: Research Lab and Training Grounds never fall far behind the Sanctuary, Walls and Infirmary catch up in blocks, and Alliance Hall gets pushed in three separate windows. If your city ever diverges wildly from this shape, that is usually the sign of a mistake — most often equally-leveling everything.

## Key Milestones: 20, 24, 27, 30 {#milestones}

The Sanctuary push is not one long grind — it is four sprints between milestones that each unlock a real power jump.

- **Sanctuary 20** — Tier 7 troops and a high-level Gear Workshop arrive together. This is the biggest single power spike of the mid-game and the first push worth burning saved speedups for.
- **Sanctuary 24, then 27** — the next tier pushes. Each one raises troop tiers and workshop ceilings again.
- **Sanctuary 30** — the cap. Everything left in the city can now be finished.

Be aware of what the late milestones cost: past the mid-20s, single upgrades run over 100 million resources. Start treating resource stockpiling as part of the build plan — see the resource scaling section below.

## Construction Speed Stacking — the Correct Math {#construction-speed}

Construction speed buffs stack from five sources, and together they compress a 38-day upgrade into 20 days (from the guide by chuppergaming). The buffs are:

| Source | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Personal Development research | +5%, then +10% at node level 5 |
| Alliance Research — Fast Building | +20% |
| VIP level 11 | +45% |
| Royal City — Minister of Construction title | +50% |

Now the part almost everyone gets wrong: these buffs apply to the **remaining time, consecutively** — not additively from the base. You cannot sum 10% + 5% + 20% + 45% + 50% and cut the timer by 130% of anything.

Here is the real arithmetic on a 37-day upgrade:

1. Start: 37 days.
2. Apply a 10% buff: remove 10% of the remaining time — 37 minus 3.7 = 33.3 days.
3. Apply a 5% buff: remove 5% of what is left — 33.3 minus 1.665 = 31.6 days.
4. Every further buff keeps shaving the remainder, never the original number.

Each additional buff is therefore worth slightly less than the one before it. The practical consequences:

- Buff order does not matter for the final result — but you must have the buffs active when the timer is long, because their value scales with the remaining time.
- Apply everything **before** starting the big Sanctuary 20+ pushes, not halfway through.
- Securing the Minister of Construction title and VIP 11 before the late push is worth days of timers by itself.

## Resource Scaling — Plan Your Stockpiles {#resource-scaling}

What a building costs at Sanctuary 8 tells you nothing about what it costs at Sanctuary 25. The curve runs in three phases:

| Phase | Typical cost per upgrade | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Early (Sanctuary 1-10) | Thousands to low millions | Production covers it. Spend freely, keep queues full. |
| Mid (Sanctuary 11-24) | Tens of millions | Plunder and events carry the load — daily plunder stops being optional. |
| Late (Sanctuary 25-30) | Over 100 million | Stockpile for days before starting. An upgrade you cannot feed stalls your whole queue. |

And keep the mix balanced: always keep Grain, Timber, and Herbs in stock. Late upgrades eat all three at once, and running dry on any of them mid-push wastes exactly the timer you stacked buffs to save.
