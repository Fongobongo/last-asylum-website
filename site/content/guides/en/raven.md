The Raven is the system that quietly separates accounts: it works like an extra hero for all your squads at once, and its epigraphs are where the mono-faction meta is actually enforced. The Raven is also the system most players neglect — a Raven stuck at level 1 is a classic mistake.

This page covers Raven levels and plans, gear strategy, epigraph rarities and the promotion order, and the rules that decide which epigraphs deserve materials.

## What the Raven Is {#what-raven-is}

The Raven is an extra hero for all your squads — its buffs apply everywhere at once, not just to one formation. Two consequences follow:

- Every point of Raven investment improves every march you field, which makes it one of the highest-value systems in the game.
- Crow Essence, its food, is the bottleneck on the entire system. Farm it early and never stop — the VIP shop and Sanctuary shop both sell it for exactly this reason.

The long-term goal is **Raven level 110**, where the final skill lands: +50% AOE damage to affected targets. That is the endgame of the system, and Crow Essence is what stands between you and it.

## Raven Levels and Plans {#raven-levels}

Raven levels unlock the epigraph slots (called Plans) one at a time:

| Raven level | Unlock |
|---|---|
| 10 | Epigraphs + Plan 1 (first slot) |
| 150 | Plan 2 (+1 system bonus) |
| 300 | Plan 3 (+2) |
| 450 | Plan 4 (+3) |
| 600 | Plan 5 (+4) |

Read the table as the Raven's true progression path: level 10 opens the whole epigraph system, and every 150 levels after that adds another slot to fill. Because each Plan slot carries its own epigraph buff, a stalled Raven level caps how many of those buffs you can even equip.

## Raven Gear Strategy {#raven-gear}

Push Raven gear to level 8 — that is where the percentage buffs arrive, and percentage buffs are what make the gear worth wearing.

Side strategy, left versus right:

- **Left side** — HP and crit-damage-reduction. Easier to acquire, defensive value.
- **Right side** — ATK and crit-damage. The best offensive value in the system.

And one calendar rule: **save your gear chests for Alliance Duel days.** Opening them then earns Alliance Duel points — the same double-dip logic that governs speedups in the [Economy guide](/economy/).

## Epigraphs: The Core Rules {#epigraph-basics}

Epigraphs slot into the Raven's Plans and buff the heroes of a faction. The rarities behave differently, and the differences drive every upgrade decision:

- **UR (gold)** — buffs ONE faction only. The end goal, and the reason mono-faction squads are the meta.

- **SR (blue) and R (green)** — buff ALL factions, but cannot be promoted. They are the everyone-gets-a-cut tier, useful while you build toward UR.

The single most important rule: **only upgrade epigraphs of your main squad's faction.** A UR epigraph that buffs a faction you do not field is worth nothing to you — and in the mono-faction meta, off-faction materials are exactly what the feed stock is for (see Quick Add below).

### The Early Meta Epigraphs {#early-epigraphs}

| Epigraph | Slot type | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Frenzy Flock | Attack | ATK buff to Warrior allies after the Raven skill fires, plus more Raven targets. |
| Reversal Power | Support | Damage for the 3 highest-ATK Warrior allies, and reduces enemy ultimate damage. |
| Protective Charm | — | Survivability pick — the standard third choice when your squad keeps dying instead of losing on damage. |

## The Epigraph Workshop {#epigraph-workshop}

Crafting UR epigraphs requires **Workshop level 20**. Everything below that is building toward it.

The rarity ladder, and what each color is actually for:

| Color | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Material | The feedstock tier. |
| Blue | Improved material | An SR blue with 2 stars performs roughly like an SSR purple — use blues until you outgrow them. |
| Purple | Transition tier | Good enough to fight with while your URs grow. |
| Gold (UR) | End goal | The target of the entire system. |

One absolute rule: **never recycle a golden epigraph unless you are certain.** UR epigraphs are the scarcest permanent asset in the game — a recycled UR is not coming back.

## Epigraph Promotion Order {#promotion-order}

Follow this order (from DrAction), promoting one slot at a time:

1. Mid — Defense slot (damage reduction).
2. Top — Attack slot (Raven Target).
3. Right — Support slot (damage reduction).
4. Left — Initiative slot (Defense% buff).

For Warrior sword epigraphs specifically, the community variant: Support (right) UR first, then Attack (top), then Initiative or Defense.

## Rarity Comparisons That Surprise People {#rarity-comparisons}

Star level can beat rarity in the damage slot. These are the verified comparisons players argue about most:

- A purple at 4 stars can outdamage a yellow (UR) at 0 stars in the attack slot — the community shorthand is purple DMG all the way.
- A shield UR at 0 stars is better than an SSR at 5 stars.
- Purple outperforms UR against King Blight until the UR reaches 3 stars or better.

The takeaway is not that purple is secretly the best tier — it is that a high-star epigraph of the right type beats a fresh UR of the wrong one. Star your epigraphs before you chase their rarity ceiling.

## SSR, UR, and What Actually Upgrades {#ssr-vs-ur}

A common misunderstanding worth killing early: you **cannot upgrade an SSR epigraph into a UR**. What you upgrade is the Plan — the slot the epigraph sits in. SSR epigraphs are placeholders in that progression, not embryos.

Which leads to the most common Raven mistake in the game: players hoard non-main-faction epigraphs instead of feeding them to the Raven via **Quick Add** — and then wonder why their Raven is stuck at level 1. Off-faction and low-rarity epigraphs are Raven food. Quick Add them; that is what they are for.
