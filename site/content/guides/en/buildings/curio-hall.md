## Tools

Curio Hall shows the Curio collection: 31 artifact structures with permanent bonuses, raised with shards. Several Curios repeat each other in numbers, and five different ones give the identical hero HP bonus. The building unlocks after Sanctuary level 8 and goes up to level 1. Only one can be built.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Unlocks at | Sanctuary level 8 |
| How many can be built | 1 |
| Max level | 1 |

## Full upgrade

Taking the building from level 1 to 1 costs Grain 480, Timber 360. Pure build time without speed-ups: under a minute.

This building gives no stat bonus: it unlocks an option rather than percentages.

## How to use it

The Curio Hall is the only instant building that brings 200 Might instead of the usual 100, though at 480 Grain and 360 Timber that difference changes nothing. For comparison, the Watchtower from the same Sanctuary level 8 reaches 311,100 Might, and its full upgrade takes 350 days. The other neighbor at that level, the second march queue, needs 299 days. The hall is built in seconds and never enters that queue.

The value of the hall is the collection itself: 31 Curios with permanent bonuses, each existing in a single copy, and a placed one cannot be removed. Half of them repeat their neighbors in numbers, and five different Curios give the identical hero HP bonus, so the hall window exists to keep a player from buying twice what already works.

Hence the timing: the hall goes up before the first large shard spending rather than after it. Resources at Sanctuary level 8 belong to the Watchtower, and the 840 resources of the hall play no part in that budget.

The 840-resource price of the hall is not its own: the Monument, the Nomad Trader and the Residence cost exactly as much. The four window buildings share a price and differ only in the Sanctuary level that opens them: 1, 6, 8 and 9. Their order is set by which system is needed earlier, not by the cost. The hall has a single level that cannot be raised: all 200 Might arrive at once, in the minute it is built.
