Era of Revival is the seasonal layer that arrives around day 120 — a roughly two-month cycle that adds its own progression systems on top of the base game: the Lord Statue (Resistance and Lord Evolution), Specialty trees, and Elite Blight.

The season rewards focus, and it punishes distraction harder than the base game does. This page covers what to prioritize daily, which specialty tree matches your spending level, what to hoard before the season starts, and the complete math of the Claire SSR-to-UR conversion.

## Season Overview {#season-overview}

One season (Expedition League) runs for **55 days** (reduced from 56 days in the August 13, 2026 update). Within it, three systems carry the progression:

- **Lord Statue** — Resistance and Lord Evolution. The backbone of the season; see the [Lord Statues section](#lord-statues).
- **Specialty trees** — role-defining bonuses, split between growth and war paths.
- **Elite Blight** — group content with Resistance-gated difficulty.

## Resistance: The #1 Daily Priority {#resistance-first}

From day 1 of the season, Resistance is the top daily priority — ahead of anything else on your list. The reason is simple: it is the first progression wall, and **all season progression depends on Resistance XP**. Every other seasonal system unlocks or scales behind it.

Two practical rules for pushing Resistance:

- **Attack monsters even at a 30% damage penalty.** The fight is often still clearable — and the Resistance XP flows either way.
- **Rally Elite Blight with a higher-Resistance ally.** Group difficulty scales with the participants; a stronger ally drags your clear along with them.

One freebie worth grabbing immediately: the **Greenery Shelter skin grants +100 Resistance XP**. Claim it, apply it, and let it pay out every single day of the season — that is 100 XP per day you do not have to fight for. The Era Shop also features the permanent **Castle of Revival** castle skin.

## Specialty Trees: Growth vs War {#specialty-trees}

The specialty trees split by spending profile, and the community picks are unambiguous:

| Profile | Trees | What they give |
|---|---|---|
| F2P and mid-spenders | Handyman / Skilled Craftsman | Research and building time cuts — rush your Sanctuary and reach Tier 10 troops faster. |
| Whales / PvP-focused | Legion Commander / Military Commander | Battle buffs, healing, and rally power — the war path. |

> [!NOTE] Specialty Tech Requirements (July 2026 update)
> Unlocking advanced Specialty Tech branches requires **Research Lab Lv. 30** and completing **80% of Elite Troop Tech**.

The logic: if you are not outspending your neighbors, the growth trees win you the season by letting you out-build them. If you already out-spend them, the war trees convert that into battlefield results.

### War Supply: The Plunder Cap Skill {#war-supply}

The Legion Commander tree carries War Supply, which raises your daily plunder limit by +6 / +14 / +24 / +36 / +50% at skill levels 1 through 5. For context on what that is worth per day, see the plunder limits table in the [Economy guide](/economy/) — at late-game caps it is millions of extra resources daily.

## Point-by-Point Builds (40 specialty points) {#specialty-builds}

You start the season with ~40 specialty points. These are the two tested builds (courtesy Korpez Gaming):

**Handyman (Growth Path)** — The mandatory path for F2P and moderate spenders. Pick up every **HP shield, +2h building speedup, construction speed** node first, then **research speed, speedups 20m, research −20 sec duration**, followed by key utility unlocks (cat + dog, battle slots, wall durability). Cap it with the two powerful active skills: **Instant Construction** (instantly completes 490 minutes of building) and **Instant Research** (−180 min research, 3-day cooldown). This path shaves hundreds of hours off construction/research and rushes you toward T10 troops weeks faster.

**Legion Commander (PvP Path)** — Tailored for rally leaders and heavy spenders. Focus on **hospital cap + (16, 12, 14, 18 ×500)**, **+3% heal speed**, **marching speed for gather/tree nodes**, then attack nodes: **attack leading 1–3%**, **Swordsmanship leading damage**, and active skills: **Siege War Flag (+100 destruction for 30 min, 24h CD)**, **Poison Pouch**, **Firecracker**, **Speed Teacher**, **Bound Shield**, **War Frenzy (leadership attack buff)**, and **All-Terrain Tactics**.

**War Frenzy trick**: attacking **any** player city (even an empty one) activates War Frenzy — the buff also applies to world bosses and the [Pandemic Experience](/events/pandemic-experience/) event. Cheap pre-boss buffing.

## Lord Statues and the Era Academy {#lord-statues}

The Lord Statues live in the Era Academy, and the two systems inside them do different jobs:

- **Resistance** — the seasonal wall. XP-gated, fed by daily play, and the gate behind which the season's content and rewards sit.
- **Lord Evolution** — permanent stat growth for your march, fed by season materials.

What resets at season end: the seasonal progression systems reset with the Era — Resistance progress and the seasonal tree climb go back to zero, while your permanent investments (Lord Evolution stats, heroes, gear) carry forward. Plan your spending accordingly: spend on permanent power freely, but do not hoard seasonal currencies past the season that issued them.

## Lord Evolution: Which Stats to Take {#lord-evolution-stats}

| Profile | Stats |
|---|---|
| F2P / progression | Research Speed, Construction Speed, Resource Production, Healing |
| PvP | Attack, March Size, Hero Damage |

The same logic as the specialty trees, one level down: progression accounts compound everything with speed stats; PvP accounts buy the stats that win fights on contact.

## Hero Progression in the Season {#hero-progression-order}

The Awakened hero ladder inside the season, for your main hero, in order:

1. Main hero to 10 stars.
2. Awakening.
3. Exclusive Weapon (reaching 10★ unlocks **Smelt** to convert excess shards into weapon enhancement materials).
4. Lord Evolution.
5. Lord Resistance.
6. Era Enhancement — one main skin only.
7. Then the second hero, through the same ladder.

Two binding rules: **never split Awakening materials**, and remember that one strong march beats several half-finished ones — the ladder exists precisely to stop you from leveling two heroes in parallel.

## What to Hoard Before the Season {#hoarding-list}

If your account is approaching day 120, this is the stockpile list. Every item here is worth more spent inside the season than before it:

- Diamonds.
- Recruit Tickets.
- Hero Fragments and Medals.
- All Speedups, including Research Speedups.
- Antitoxin.
- Raven Fruit.
- Resources.
- Weapon Materials.

The pattern: the season adds new ways to spend all of them, and several seasonal events reward consumption — hoarding converts directly into event points and progression once the Era starts.

## Week 1 Purchases (Spenders) {#week-1-purchases}

- **Battle Pass + Weekly Era Pass** — +250 Resistance and Specialty XP. Best value timing of the season: buy them week 1 so the passes pay out across the full two months.
- **Hold the Exclusive Weapon and Awaken Shard packs** until the Exclusive Pass Event is active. Same packs, more value when the event pays you for buying them.

## Claire SSR-to-UR Conversion (Era Day 8) {#claire-conversion}

The Claire conversion opens at Era Day 8 — but do not touch it on day 8 without reading this first. The conversion lands Claire at 6 stars UR, and the road from there to 10 is where the problems live.

### The Costs {#conversion-costs}

- Claire eats **SSR shards, not UR shards** — the hoarded early-game shards are exactly what this conversion was designed around.
- Every substage costs **x2**: going 6 stars to 10 stars takes 1,600 shards total.

### The Gains {#conversion-gains}

| Star level | Damage vs maxed SSR |
|---|---|
| 9 stars | ~1.70x |
| 10 stars | ~2.20x |

### The Passive Dip {#the-passive-dip}

The trap: her passive team buff drops when you convert. The SSR's maxed passive is a 16% team buff; at 6 stars UR it is only 10% — a real couple of percent team loss while you climb back. It recovers at 9 stars (14%) and overtakes at 10 stars (22%).

### Skipping the Dip: The Hall of Honor Strategy {#skip-the-dip}

You can skip the dip entirely by preparing Hall of Honor levels **before** converting:

| Hall of Honor level | Conversion outcome |
|---|---|
| Lv. 60 | Claire lands at 8 stars — Advanced Tenacity (+20% ATK / HP / DEF, +10% cooldown speed) |
| Lv. 100 | Claire lands at 9 stars |
| Lv. 160 | All 1,600 shards banked — instant 10 stars, no dip at all |

The bottom line on timing: for an account without a full UR line-up, this conversion is a huge PvP upgrade — a 2.20x damage carry built on shards you hoarded for free. Just time it against your Hall of Honor level and shard bank so you land at 8 stars or better, not at 6.

## Era Season Systems: Contest, Resistance Chain, Marlena, Daskal, Claire {#era-season-systems}

What launch confirmed (DTF season guide). The Era is a two-month seasonal system where daily discipline beats raw power. The biggest start mistake is pressing everything at once: wrong specialization, diamonds into the roulette, energy into weak mobs, missed weapon-fragment sources.

**Era Contest: mass activity required.** The headline event pits 32 servers in two large groups across two months, with personal, alliance and server rewards. Alliances score during the throne battle in KvK: the higher alliance members place, the more the common pool gets — and the winning side earns more points for the same placement than the losing side. Just "placing" is not enough; the server must actually fight. Agree inside the alliance who plays actively and when: in these seasons chaos kills progress harder than a weak roster.

**Threat Elimination: seasonal mobs and Resistance.** During the Era, regular resource mobs and elite blights are replaced by seasonal ones, and ranking goes to whoever kills higher-level mobs first. Each mob has an infection level countered by Resistance — a strong squad without enough Resistance stalls on a high mob. Resistance is the key to stronger mobs, better ranking and seasonal progress: start it early. Do not burn energy on the nearest mobs; check which levels are available, where the rewards are better, what the ranking needs, and whether Resistance covers it — energy drains fast in the Era.

**Lord Statue: the engine.** The new Lord Statue building holds Resistance and Specialization. Resistance grows on experience that the statue itself accumulates and must be collected like a resource — and hourly XP income scales with Specialization level. The chain runs: Specialization → more Resistance XP → stronger seasonal hunting → more progress. Never leave the statue "for later."

![Lord Statue with Resistance and Specialization](/img/dtf/era/05.jpg)

**Specialization: Handyman or Legion Commander — no tourism.** Two branches, one choice, switching through a reset book that may be rare at launch. Handyman is the progress branch: daily-activity value, faster specialization growth, better monster farming and stamina use across the two-month season — the calm default for F2P and low-spend. Legion Commander is the combat branch for regular KvK, throne and seasonal-clash fighters at the top of the alliance. If in doubt, take Handyman: early Era needs Resistance ramp, seasonal bonuses and stable farming first; war without a seasonal foundation ends in "came, got carried out." Specialization levels on XP books from mobs, Alliance Duel checkpoints, the Era Weekly Pass, the Era Covenant pass, and throne-war activities.

Why rush it — the unlock ladder: level 10 gives one free direct relocation per day (does not stack — use it); level 15 opens exclusive-weapon fragments in Survival Battle rewards (from Era day 8); level 20 adds fragments in Kingdom War win streaks; level 25 opens VIP-shop access; level 35 alliance shop; level 40 sanctuary shop. The strongest spending is the kind that accelerates the whole season toward a fragment threshold, not one battle.

**Marlena Awakening: regular shards do not count.** The first awakened hero is Marlena, and awakening needs separate awakening fragments — 50 individual Marlena awakening fragments to start, then individual or universal fragments per further level. Regular Marlena shards go to stars only. Spend universals carefully: they are likely the scarcest resource of the new meta with more awakenings coming. Sources: the pass ticket launching with the Era (Marlena personals), Honor and Expedition shops plus Alliance Duel checkpoints 6 and 9 (universals), and from week two Marlena personals in the daily deals alongside hero shards. Awakening raises skill-level caps and adds effects: Battle Will stacks on crits (more Warrior teammates raise the stack cap) for damage and survivability, a team ATK/HP/DEF boost to allied Warriors, enemy weakening at high stacks, an opener against highest-HP enemies, and extra sword hits on the ultimate. Even awakening level 1 tested as a visible damage gain for boss and thick-target PvE — but results depend on team, stars, gear and fit. If Marlena is your main damage, awakening is near-mandatory over time; if she stands on the side of another faction's core, do not chase hype before you can build around her.

**Era passes.** Two stand out: the Era Covenant (long pass, faster specialization) and the Weekly Era Pass (specialization XP, +5% march speed, some Resistance). Neither promises a miracle; both accelerate a concrete system, which makes them the sane purchase shape — the Weekly relatively nicer for daily mob grinders.

**Era bonuses: build a separate PvE squad.** Claire, Marlena, Cynthia and Annie each grant +5% monster damage per such hero in the squad; Daskal, Louis and Ulfrid each reduce monster damage taken by 5%. Against an over-level seasonal mob these decide the fight — sometimes the right pick is the hero with the seasonal effect, not the higher tier-list name.

![Era hero bonuses against monsters](/img/dtf/era/06.jpg)

**Era shop: buy long-term power first.** The image shop is currently a display case (e.g. a 40,000-coupon squirrel skin with a pleasant +100 Resistance during the Era — not for everyone). The Era shop holds red blueprints, the Epoch Beacon relic, specialization-change books, skill-reset books and extra specialization skill points. For non-whales the call is red blueprints first: they raise gear to 5 stars, which is stable long-term progress. Golden rule: what strengthens the account for longest, first.

![Era shop with red blueprints and specialization books](/img/dtf/era/07.jpg)

**Daskal's Exclusive Weapon: separate equipment.** Arriving in Era week two, it unlocks on Daskal's individual weapon fragments and levels on individual or universal fragments — and it does not replace the hero's normal weapon, it works alongside current gear. Star milestones: 4★ stronger bonus damage from Daskal's first skill; 6★ a shield to the support hero when HP falls below 50%; 8★ +400% healing from the second skill; 10★ +30% absorbing shield on the ultimate. Universal weapon fragments also come from play: the last Survival Battle chest, Kingdom War win streaks, VIP, alliance and sanctuary shops — but many sources gate on Specialization level. Want the weapon — level the statue.

**Claire turns UR: free legendary, not a win button.** In Era week two, old SSR Claire can elevate to UR through a medal earned on Resistance progress — no paywall on the opportunity itself, which makes it genuinely good F2P news. After elevation she leaves the Hall of Fame, her skills reset, stars land around level 6, part of the Hall investment returns by mail (check letters), and further stars cost roughly double. Expect her to look weaker than the old maxed SSR at first: skills reset, stars lower, the passive must be rebuilt — restore the key passive before judging damage. She stays a PvE hero tuned for monster damage and Warrior support, best beside Marlena and strong Warriors against seasonal mobs and the Demon King — a long-term PvE upgrade, not instant PvP dominance.

![Claire's elevation to UR legendary](/img/dtf/era/08.jpg)

**Tower to 60 and old activities matter again.** Expedition Tower cap rises to 60, and 50+ gold chests can hold universal exclusive-weapon fragments. New Demon King damage checkpoints arrive and cheese traps get stronger; the Survival Battle 18-point last chest now ties into weapon progress — finishing dailies stops being optional.

![Expedition Tower level 60](/img/dtf/era/09.jpg)

**Soldier Shelter.** The new building takes in part of the irreversibly lost soldiers; they recover over time and return to barracks. It softens mistakes across a long season and KvK, but losses stay losses — it is insurance, not a license for headless war. Note: hero awakening and exclusive weapons persist after the Era — long-term systems, not a two-month attraction.

![Soldier Shelter recovers part of fallen soldiers](/img/dtf/era/10.jpg)

**Two economy notes.** Daskal and Lady enter the regular tavern (first server groups first, then waves) — a long F2P path via saved tickets, best combined with ticket-spend events rather than pulled on sight. And the fourth roulette, Marlena's (fragments, epigraph chests, XP signs at 9,000 diamonds per 10 pulls), is dubious at Marlena 10 stars and for F2P/low-spend: with the Era pulling resources from every side, a purposeless roulette is an emotional diamond drain.

**F2P order.** Resistance and specialization first (the season's base); then old dailies every day — Survival Battle, Alliance Duel, Expedition Tower, seasonal mobs (they now feed new systems); then heroes by role (Marlena awakening if she is the carry, Claire rebuild for Warrior PvE, Daskal's weapon if he is the main tank); Era shop with discipline (red blueprints); spending decisions last, each tied to a named task — pass, specialization, Marlena, Claire, weapon, or KvK prep.

## Era Development Tips: Resistance First, No Scattering {#era-dev-tips}

Short practice add-on to the two sections above (DTF tips post) — read this for order, read them for mechanics.

**Resistance before everything.** The Lord Statue is the season's main button and Resistance (RES) is its first dial: it decides whether resource monsters and Elite Blight above your weight are even hittable. Late-Era gates get serious (around RES Lv.40 and ~10,000 RES readings), so do not postpone it — raise RES in parallel with everything else and never let seasonal resources scatter. First days: any RES raise on sight goes in; everything else is pleasant but optional, because without RES the content door stays shut.

**Branch by account, default Handyman.** The specialization hall offers Handyman or Legion Commander — choose by task, not icon. Handyman fits a calm start, especially spend-free: buildings, research and steady seasonal growth, with Resistance XP income as an early payoff (hourly RES Level EXP readings up to ~43,200/hr cited). Legion Commander fits active PvP and alliance battles. Unsure — start Handyman: economy and research first, war later.

**Mobs: test small penalties, rally Elites.** Both Resource Monsters and Elite Blight key off RES, and missing RES cuts damage — but a red penalty is not an automatic skip. Around −30% damage, a well-built squad above the other minimums often still clears a nearby-level target: one probe beats guessing. For Elite Blight the logic flips — it is an alliance target, so rally it with whoever already holds the needed RES instead of waiting for everyone to catch up individually.

![Resource monsters and Elite Blight RES requirements](/img/dtf/era/11.jpg)

**Passes in order.** For a faster season look at the Era Weekly Pass first — mainly for the extra RES that unlocks stronger targets earlier — plus Era XP, diamonds, resources and march-speed bonus. The Era Covenant (season battle pass with an extra reward line) complements it, and pays proportionally to how actively seasonal tasks are cleared.

![Era Weekly Pass and Era Covenant rewards](/img/dtf/era/12.jpg)

**Day-one checklist.** Lord Statue first (RES + specialization track), collect Resistance XP instead of letting it sit, take Handyman for most accounts, spend energy climbing toward the highest confidently clearable mob level rather than farming the nearest weak ones, run Elite Blight through the alliance, and only then consider season purchases (Weekly for RES acceleration, Covenant for daily grinders). The Era rewards the disciplined finger, not the fastest one.
