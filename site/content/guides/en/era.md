Era of Revival is the seasonal layer that arrives around day 120 — a roughly two-month cycle that adds its own progression systems on top of the base game: the Lord Statue (Resistance and Lord Evolution), Specialty trees, and Elite Blight.

The season rewards focus, and it punishes distraction harder than the base game does. This page covers what to prioritize daily, which specialty tree matches your spending level, what to hoard before the season starts, and the complete math of the Claire SSR-to-UR conversion.
