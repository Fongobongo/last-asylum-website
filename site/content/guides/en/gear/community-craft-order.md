## Community Craft Order (DTF Practice) {#dtf-craft-order}

This practice guide crafts by role, not by portrait: a main DPS, a second DPS, a main tank, a second tank and a support. Who exactly holds each job depends on the account (Marlena, Red Lady or another carry; Arthur, Daskal, Louis or another front) — the function decides the item.

![Gear guide: who gets what first](/img/dtf/gear/01.jpg)

### Purple gear is not trash {#dtf-purple-base}

Do not treat purple gear as junk until a gold replacement exists. The rule is simple: **the main five must always be dressed** — not perfectly, not instantly gold, but with no empty slots, because empty slots are lost stats that quickly become "why did I lose again". Second and third squads can keep purples; gold resources stay with the main squad until it stands.

![Purple and gold hero gear](/img/dtf/gear/02.jpg)

### Squad roles for this chapter {#dtf-squad-roles}

1. Main DPS — most of the damage. 2. Second DPS — finishes and pressures the backline. 3. Main tank — takes the hit. 4. Second tank — keeps the front from collapsing. 5. Support — heals, buffs, saves.

![Main squad with roles](/img/dtf/gear/03.jpg)

### First gold: main-carry sword {#dtf-swords-first}

The common first mistake is starting with the tank. Without damage, the tank just watches a longer loss. So item one is a **gold sword for the main damage dealer**: it scales the carry's whole point plus crit, and fights are often decided by whoever breaks the enemy backline first — not by the fattest shield.

![Gold sword for the main carry as craft one](/img/dtf/gear/04.jpg)

Item two stays on damage: **sword for the second damage dealer**. One carry can be switched off; two add stability, finishing power and skill value.

### Next: gloves for both damage dealers {#dtf-gloves-next}

Items three and four are **gloves (main, then second)** — attack potential plus useful stage bonuses that stack with what the swords already give. Two swords and two gloves form the first real attacking frame.

![Sword to 20 and gloves to 10 for damage dealers](/img/dtf/gear/05.jpg)

### Why armor goes to damage dealers before tanks {#dtf-armor-dd-first}

Tanks have more base HP and defense; thin damage dealers die before their key skill. So items five and six are **armor for the main and second DPS** (survive to cast, crit and tear the target), and only then armor for the two tanks (seven, eight). The front matters — but damage must get its chance first.

### Boots: energy resistance for the core four {#dtf-boots-energy}

Boots at key levels give **energy damage resistance**, which battle reports show hitting both back and front lines from enemy carry skills. Craft order: boots for main DPS, second DPS, main tank, second tank (nine–twelve).

![SSR armor and boots for protection](/img/dtf/gear/06.jpg)

### Then tank gloves and tank swords {#dtf-tank-gloves-swords}

After the core four have boots: gloves for both tanks (thirteen, fourteen), then swords for both tanks (fifteen, sixteen). Tank swords are not first, but they are not useless late — stats count in the endgame, and some tank skills tie into damage or recovery.

### Support last — not least {#dtf-support-last}

Support does not race for the first sword because its job is surviving, casting and buffing. Order: **armor → boots → gloves → sword** (seventeen–twenty). Armor and boots keep it alive, gloves add parameters, the sword is the final touch once the core four are dressed.

![Role-based gear craft order](/img/dtf/gear/07.jpg)

### Level thresholds: 20 first, then 40 {#dtf-level-thresholds}

Crafting is only the start. First real gate: **main-carry sword to 20** (crit bonus the whole sword project exists for), then main gloves to 10; repeat for the second carry (sword 20, gloves 10) — the first attacking minimum. Do not wander to tank armor, support boots and second-tank swords in between, or nothing spikes.

Next survivability comes from **boots to 20** (energy resistance) in order main DPS → second DPS → main tank → second tank → support. Then **armor to 20** (main tank, second tank, main DPS, second DPS, support — adjust by reports: backline dying fast → DPS armor earlier; front melting → tanks first).

Then the adult phase: carry swords toward the current star cap (main first, then second), then DPS gloves, then defense. **Boots of the core four to 40** is one of the best defensive steps against energy metas — heroes live to cast one more skill, which can decide the fight. Then **tank armor to 40** (physical bonuses for the front line), DPS armor (30 for base stats and HP on the way, 40 in perspective). Support: boots to 20/40, armor to 20/40, gloves, sword last.

### Stars: where the real pain starts {#dtf-star-order}

Stars unlock after the Gear Workshop hits level 20 and run on blueprints: 1 star = 5, 2 stars = +10, 3 stars = +15, 4 stars = +20 (**50 blue per item to 4 stars**); the 5th star needs red blueprints — a luxury project for items that will live on the main hero long (main DPS first, then second DPS, then team defense).

![Workshop and Honor Shop blueprints](/img/dtf/gear/08.jpg)

Star order: **2 stars on the main-carry sword** (gain plus higher cap) → 1 star main gloves → 2 stars second-carry sword → 1 star second gloves → main sword toward 4 stars → core-four boots to 2 stars → DPS gloves, armor, tanks, and 5-star keys. Sword effects scale battle damage; armor helps vs crits; boots cut crit damage; gloves add damage and resistance — but reds are not party candy.

### Where blueprints come from {#dtf-blueprint-sources}

Blueprints tie to the Honor Shop and medal events (Arena, Elixir, Kingdom War, Canyon, Crystal Valley). Never skip Honor-currency events even when blueprints feel unneeded — they become the narrowest bottleneck later. Pick the item first, then gather for it.

### One strong squad beats two half-built {#dtf-one-squad}

Throne, tower, castle, Arena, Elixir, Canyon, Crystal Valley: the first squad must break the opponent, and a second average squad does not save it. Max the main squad first, then the second, then a third. Craft, level and star cheatsheets:

Craft: sword main DPS → sword second DPS → gloves main → gloves second → armor main DPS → armor second DPS → armor main tank → armor second tank → boots ×4 → tank gloves ×2 → tank swords ×2 → support armor/boots/gloves/sword.

![Gold gear craft scheme for five heroes](/img/dtf/gear/09.jpg)

Levels: sword main to 20 → gloves main to 10 → sword second to 20 → gloves second to 10 → core-four boots to 20 → tank and DPS armor to 20 → DPS swords to cap → core-four boots to 40 → tank armor to 40 → DPS gloves/armor on → support last.

![Level cheatsheet](/img/dtf/gear/10.jpg)

Stars: 2★ sword main → 1★ gloves main → 2★ sword second → 1★ gloves second → sword main to 4★ → core-four boots to 2★ → DPS gloves, armor, tanks, 5★ keys.

If in doubt: does this raise my main damage or help the main squad live to its skills? A glowing red dot is not your boss.
