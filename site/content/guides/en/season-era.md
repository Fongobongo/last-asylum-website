The Era of Revival runs for 56 days, and a new one is announced 5 days before it starts. The game calls it the Era of Revival, and players shorten it to the Era or the Age. Everything inside an Age counts from the Age day rather than from server age, so the whole schedule shifts with the start of each new Age. The opening move is the Lord Statue, because the chain from it leads to Specialization and Resistance.

## Open for the whole Age

Age Contest, Age Covenant (battle pass), Age Bonus, Lord Specialization, Resistance, the Weekly Pass and the Expedition League run from day 1 to the end. Everything else arrives on schedule and stays open for a limited time.

## Age heroes by week

Hero windows open one per week and last 7 days each, so a missed window waits until the next Age.

| Age day | What opens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Marlena Awakening |
| 8 | Daskal Exclusive Weapon |
| 15 | Cynthia Awakening |
| 22 | Louis Exclusive Weapon |
| 29 | Annie Awakening |
| 36 | Ulfrid Exclusive Weapon |

Hero Promotion stands apart from that rhythm: it opens on day 8 and runs 49 days, almost to the end of the Age.

## Where to start

The Lord Statue comes first: it unlocks Lord Specialization, which in turn grants Resistance. Specialization has 40 levels, and each one gives a skill point.

## Resistance only works up to the enemy level

The difference is the enemy Resistance minus the player's own. When the player's is higher, the difference falls to zero and nobody receives a bonus. Catching up is worth the effort, overshooting is not.

## Weekly Pass

The pass lasts 7 days and grants Resistance +250 and March Speed +5% while it is active. Each day it hands over 20,000 specialization XP, 100 diamonds, a stamina item and 80 five-minute speed-ups.

## Season Practice Notes: Resistance, Specialization, Awakening, Weapons, Claire {#season-practice-notes}

Practice companion to the schedule above (DTF season notes; full mechanics live in the [Era guide](/era/)). No duplication here — only what the schedule does not say.

**Resistance gates the hunt.** Seasonal mobs carry an infection level countered by Resistance, and late gates run toward RES Lv.40 / ~10,000 RES. A red −30% penalty is a probe, not a skip: a well-built squad near the target level often still clears. Run Elite Blight as rallies with whoever already holds the needed RES instead of waiting for individual catch-up.

![Resource monsters and Elite Blight RES requirements](/img/dtf/era/11.jpg)

**Specialization is the engine behind Resistance.** The Lord Statue accumulates Resistance XP hourly, and income scales with Specialization level — so the statue comes first and the branch choice matters. Handyman by default (economy, research, steady growth; hourly RES EXP payoffs cited up to ~43,200/hr); Legion Commander only for regular PvP fighters. The unlock ladder to memorize: 10 (daily free teleport, use it), 15 (exclusive-weapon fragments in Survival Battle from day 8), 20 (Kingdom War streaks), 25 (VIP shop), 35 (alliance shop), 40 (sanctuary shop).

**Marlena Awakening splits materials.** Start costs 50 individual Marlena awakening fragments; further levels take individual or universal fragments, while regular Marlena shards stay on stars. Universals are the scarcest line (Honor/Expedition shops, Alliance Duel checkpoints 6 and 9; Marlena personals join daily deals in week two) — spend on emotion and the next hero has nothing. Awakened Marlena centers a Warrior squad: Battle Will stacks on crits (Warrior count raises the cap), team ATK/HP/DEF, weakening at high stacks, an opener into highest-HP enemies, extra ultimate sword hits.

**Daskal's weapon is separate gear.** Week-two exclusive weapon unlocks on Daskal individual fragments and fights alongside the normal weapon: 4★ first-skill damage, 6★ support shield under 50% HP, 8★ second-skill healing +400%, 10★ ultimate +30% absorbing shield. Universal weapon fragments flow from the Survival Battle last chest, Kingdom War streaks, VIP/alliance/sanctuary shops — most gated on Specialization level.

**Claire elevation is free but humbling.** The week-two medal (Resistance progress) turns old SSR Claire into UR: she leaves the Hall of Fame, skills reset, stars land near 6 with partial Hall refunds by mail, and further stars cost about double. She looks weaker than the old maxed SSR until the key passive is rebuilt — restore first, judge after. Role stays PvE Warrior support (seasonal mobs, Demon King), best beside Marlena.

![Claire's elevation to UR legendary](/img/dtf/era/08.jpg)

**Old activities feed new systems.** Expedition Tower cap 60 (50+ gold chests hold universal weapon fragments), new Demon King damage checkpoints, and the Survival Battle 18-point chest tied to weapon progress. The Soldier Shelter banks part of irreversibly lost troops and trickles them back — insurance across a two-month season, while awakening and weapons persist past the Era. Full detail, shop priorities (red blueprints first) and the F2P order live in the [Era guide](/era/).
