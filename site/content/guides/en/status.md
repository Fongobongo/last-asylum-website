## Scam alert (important) {#scam-alert}

Scammers impersonate officials to steal accounts. Real staff **never** ask for passwords, verification codes, or payments outside official channels. Enable Facebook/Google account linking in Settings.

## Can't log in? Quick fixes {#login-fixes}

- Restart the game fully
- Clear the game cache (may require re-login)
- Disable VPN if you use one
- Use the **Repair** button (top-right corner on the loading screen)
