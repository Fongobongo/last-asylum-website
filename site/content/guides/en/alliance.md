In the plague-torn wastelands of **Last Asylum: Plague**, surviving as a solo player is nearly impossible. Joining a powerful, well-structured Alliance speeds up your account progression by **3x to 5x**, unlocks exclusive shops, shields your resources, and opens the door to high-stakes cross-server warfare.

---

## 1. Joining an Alliance & Early Steps

The Alliance feature unlocks at **Sanctuary Level 5**.

### How to Find the Right Alliance:
1. Tap the **[Alliance]** icon in the bottom-right corner of your main screen.
2. Avoid joining random "Open" beginner guilds. Competitive, organized alliances always require an application review (Apply).
3. **Key Selection Criteria:**
   * **Might Ranking:** Strive to enter a Top 1 to Top 3 guild on your server. They dominate the Royal Castle, hold the Ruling Guild title, and generate thousands of gift chests from active members.
   * **Timezone & Chat Activity:** Ensure the guild's prime raiding hours align with your schedule for Cheese Trap and Undead Siege rallies.
   * **Gift Level:** Higher alliance gift tiers drop better milestone chests containing free diamonds, recruit tickets, and speedups.

---

## 2. Alliance Help Mechanics & Formulas

Every time you initiate a building upgrade, laboratory research, or hospital healing session, an alliance help handshake appears above your base.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Help Reduction Formula:**
> Each help tap from an ally trims the remaining countdown by **1% of the total base time OR 1 minute** (whichever is greater).

* The maximum number of helps you can receive is dictated by your **Alliance Hall** level. At level 25, you can receive 30 assists, knocking off up to **30% of your total construction time** for free!
* **Etiquette Rule:** Frequently tap "Help All" in your alliance interface. Every assist earns you **Alliance Honor Points**, used to buy critical supplies in the Alliance Shop.

## 3. Alliance Technology (Alliance Tech)

Alliance research provides passive buffs to every single member across military strength, gathering, and city defense.

### Donation Rules:
* 1 donation attempt regenerates every 20 minutes (capped at 20 stored attempts).
* Donate resources or diamonds into the starred priority tech chosen by officers.
* Every donation has a chance to trigger a **Critical Multiplier (x2, x5, x10)** for bonus Honor and Alliance XP.

### Officer Research Priority:
1. **Development:** "Big Family" (membership capacity) $\rightarrow$ "Mutual Aid" (boosts help timer reduction).
2. **Territory:** Gathering speed on alliance land and warehouse protection buffs.
3. **War:** Troop Attack/HP multipliers and Rally Capacity enhancements.

## 4. Alliance Gifts & The Level 25 Privilege

Whenever an alliance comrade slays a Thief Leader, defeats an Elite Blight monster, or purchases a pack, **every member receives a Gift Chest**.

* **Gift XP:** Opening chests elevates your Alliance Gift Level.
* **Level 25 Privilege (September Update):**
  Reaching Alliance Gift Lv. 25 unlocks a permanent perk: **every Regular Wagon in the Alliance Trade Caravan carries 1 additional passenger**. This yields substantial extra daily passive income in resources and shards for all members.

## 5. Alliance Shop Priority Guide

Alliance Honor earned through daily donations and assistance should be spent with care:

| Priority | Item | Strategic Value |
|---|---|---|
| **S-Tier** | **8h / 24h Peace Shield** | Mandatory for protecting troops during KvK and Kill Events. Never waste diamonds on shields! |
| **S-Tier** | **Advanced Teleport** | Instant relocation to alliance territory or boss rally markers. |
| **A-Tier** | **Talent / Skill Reset** | Swap between economic build and combat builds for Alliance Duel. |
| **B-Tier** | **Universal Speedups** | Stockpile for Friday speedup scoring phases. |
| **F-Tier** | Basic Resources (Grain, Timber) | Never buy — easily gathered from world map tiles. |

## 6. Territory & Central Fortress

* **Central Fortress:** The anchor of your guild's borders. As long as the fortress stands, the territory emits an active defensive perimeter.
* **Banners:** Expand borders outward towards high-level resource clusters.
* **Territory Buffs:**
  * +20% to +30% gathering speed within guild borders;
  * Defense buffs for member sanctuaries situated inside territory;
  * Protection against unauthorized caravan interception along guild routes.

---

## 7. Rank Structure & Officer Responsibilities (R1 – R5)

* **R5 (Guild Leader):** Strategy, server diplomacy, peace pacts, fortress placement, war declarations.
* **R4 (Officers):**
  * **War Marshal:** Leads rally calls, times city sieges, coordinates KvK defense.
  * **Goddess:** Keeps morale high, organizes internal community contests, coordinates buff cycles.
  * **Recruiter:** Screens applicants, reaches out to active map wanderers, manages roster spots.
  * **Builder:** Plans banner grids and fortress expansion.
* **R3 – R1:** Core fighters, gatherers, and recruits.
