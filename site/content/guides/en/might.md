Many commanders make a critical mistake in **Last Asylum: Plague**: they chase raw profile Might by training endless hordes of low-tier soldiers. The result is what seasoned players call **"Paper Might"** — an impressive number on your profile that collapses in seconds against a refined combat squad, sending hundreds of thousands of troops straight into permanent hospital overflow death.

Here is the breakdown of the official Might formula and the most resource-efficient growth path.

---

## 1. The Official Might Formula

Total account Might is calculated from five distinct pillars:

$$\text{Might} = \text{Heroes} + \text{Raven} + \text{Troops} + \text{Technology} + \text{Buildings}$$

### Priority Investment Hierarchy:
1. **Hero Might (S-Tier):** Directly powers your damage output, sustain, and troop leadership in all battle modes (Arena, KvK, garrisons, rallies).
2. **Raven & Epigraph Might (A-Tier):** Provides persistent global percentage buffs to all deployed units.
3. **Military Technology (A-Tier):** Multiplies ATK, DEF, and march velocity across entire unit classes.
4. **Buildings (B-Tier):** Sanctuary level dictates level caps for all other structures and unlocks higher tier systems.
5. **Troop Might (Caution!):** Only train as many top-tier troops (T7/T8/T9) as your march queues and Hospital capacity can safely accommodate. Excess troops are free points for enemy raiders during Kill Events.

---

## 2. Hero Cultivation: The 4 Pillars

Individual Hero Might follows:
$$\text{Hero Might} = \text{Level} + \text{Star Rating} + \text{Skills} + \text{Gear}$$

### 1. Hero Level & Antitoxin
Hero levels scale base attributes (HP, ATK, DEF) and amplify percentage gear stats:
* **Antitoxin:** The primary fuel for hero leveling.
* **Pro Tip:** Press and hold the [Upgrade] button to trigger smooth automatic leveling without hundreds of repetitive taps.

### 2. Star Rating Breakthroughs
* The primary inflection point for any UR hero is **4 Stars**. Reaching 4 stars unlocks game-changing team passives and substantial stat scaling.
* **F2P Golden Rule:** Prioritize taking one core carry hero (e.g., Marlena or Arthur) to 4–5 stars before spreading shards across multiple 1-star heroes.

### 3. Gear Promotion (Up to Lv. 60)
* Gear consumes Blueprints to promote at key milestones: **Lv. 10, Lv. 20, Lv. 30, Lv. 40, and Lv. 60**.
* **Upgrade Order:** Max out the weapon and accessories of your primary backline damage dealer first, then bolster your frontline tank's armor.

### 4. Exclusive Weapon Smelting (10-Star Milestone)
* Reaching 10 Stars on an Exclusive Weapon unlocks the **Smelt** mechanic, granting powerful secondary stat rolls (DEF Pierce, Crit DMG).

## 3. Top Antitoxin Sources

| Source | How to Maximize |
|---|---|
| **Antitoxin Workshop** | Keep workshop level synchronized with your Sanctuary. Collect regularly via the Stable. |
| **Building Upgrades** | Every completed structure upgrade awards an instant batch of Antitoxins. |
| **Falcon Tower Quests** | Prioritize purple and gold missions offering Antitoxin vials. |
| **Covert Ops & Caravan Plunder** | Complete daily routes and raid rival caravans (up to 3 times per day). |
| **Blight & Elite Blight Rallies** | Coordinate guild rallies against high-tier infected for steady resource flow without gem cost. |

## 4. Raven & Epigraph Progression

* **Raven Gear Fusion:** Craft and fuse gear pieces to trigger the Night Raven Mask and Raven Feather Cloak set bonuses.
* **Epigraphs (Up to 6 Stars):** Upgradable to 6 Stars. The UR "Guard of Nature" epigraph activates an opening battle shield followed by damage mitigation upon barrier destruction.
