> Data verified against the game client (v1.0.87, source: [wiki-last-asylum.com](https://wiki-last-asylum.com/en/wiki/trials)).

Trials complete themselves as ordinary progress goes on: a gear promotion, another star on an Exclusive Weapon, more epigraph Might. There are 52 tasks in all, and none of them asks a player to go anywhere.

What makes them worth watching is the currency they pay in, universal UR hero fragments. All the tasks together give 0 fragments, and score chests add more on top. Unlocking a hero costs 10 shards, and few other places in the game hand out universal fragments by the dozen.

Most of the tasks hang on Exclusive Weapon stars, and the table shows what each kind is worth.

| Task | Count |
|---|---|
| Gear Promotion | 9 |
| Exclusive Weapon reaches 1★ | 3 |
| Exclusive Weapon reaches 2★ | 3 |
| Exclusive Weapon reaches 3★ | 3 |
| Exclusive Weapon reaches 4★ | 3 |
| Exclusive Weapon reaches 5★ | 3 |
| Exclusive Weapon reaches 6★ | 3 |
| Exclusive Weapon reaches 7★ | 3 |

## Score chests

The same trials also earn points, and points open chests holding fragments and skill badges.

| Score | Chest pays |
|---|---|
| 100 | UR Hero Omni Shard 2, Skill Badge 1,250 |
| 200 | UR Hero Omni Shard 2, Skill Badge 1,250 |
| 300 | UR Hero Omni Shard 3, Skill Badge 1,500 |
| 400 | UR Hero Omni Shard 3, Skill Badge 1,500 |
| 500 | UR Hero Omni Shard 4, Skill Badge 2,000 |

Skill badges across all trials come to 42,250, a modest figure next to a full skill, yet it arrives on top of progress that was happening anyway.
