## 🧠 How to Read a Tier List Correctly {#tier-context}

A hero doesn't exist apart from the team: the same character is great in their faction squad, mediocre in a random mix and nearly useless in the wrong role. Evaluate heroes by asking:

- Who holds the frontline? Who deals the main damage? Who heals or buffs?
- Is there control, shields, defense shred or an ATK buff?
- Does the faction bonus come together? Is the hero for PvP or PvE?
- Can an F2P/low-spend player realistically build them?

Examples: Red Lady is a top carry, but in a weak squad with no frontline she dies before ramping up. Harper doesn't shine in damage numbers, but in a Warrior squad he matters more than another damage dealer. Shadow can decide a fight against certain comps, but must be placed deliberately. Bell is strong in a Ranger squad and weaker outside it; Nicole is near S-tier inside Warlocks but a late hero; Billy is great against control but more situational than universal tanks.

## 🛡️ Squad guide: reading tiers through comps {#dtf-squad-guide}

A hero doesn't live apart from the team: the same character is great in their faction squad, mediocre in a random mix, and nearly useless in the wrong role. This section reads the tier list through squads — who holds the front, who deals damage, who buffs, and which comps to actually build.

![Hero tier list and best squads overview](/img/dtf/tier-list/01.jpg)

### Faction bonuses and hybrid formats {#dtf-faction-bonus}

Faction bonuses scale with commitment:

- **3 heroes of one faction** — +5% HP/ATK/DEF.
- **3 + 2 split** — +10% HP/ATK/DEF.
- **4 heroes of one faction** — +15% HP/ATK/DEF.
- **5 heroes of one faction** — +20% HP/ATK/DEF.

![UR hero tier list by faction: Warriors, Rangers and Warlocks](/img/dtf/tier-list/02.jpg)

![Faction bonuses for 3, 3+2, 4 and 5 heroes of one faction](/img/dtf/tier-list/03.jpg)

On paper 20% looks best, but 5/5 is not always stronger: if the fifth hero only fills the slot for the bonus, a 4+1 or 3+2 with correct roles plays better. Example: four strong Warriors plus a well-built Cynthia can beat a full Warrior squad whose fifth member contributes nothing.

Faction counters matter for PvP: **Warriors take 20% less damage from Warlocks, Rangers take 20% less from Warriors, Warlocks take 20% less from Rangers.** Don't build the whole account around counter-picking — front, damage and support come first — but the matchup genuinely swings results on servers dominated by one comp.

### All 15 UR heroes by role {#dtf-ur-roster}

**Warriors:** Arthur (tank — holds the front, shields the team), Daskal (tank — shields, self-heal, second front next to Arthur), Marlena (carry — energy damage, often the main DPS), Harper (support — ATK buffs, completes the Warrior core), Zoya (carry — energy damage, crits, multi-target pressure).

**Rangers:** Red Lady (carry — hits dangerous high-ATK targets, energy damage, stuns), Cynthia (carry — energy blades, crit, multi-target), Bell (support — healing and ATK buffs that glue the Ranger squad together), Shadow (tank/debuffer — shreds enemy defense, answers energy comps), Louis (tank — self-heal and damage reduction, good vs burst/energy).

**Warlocks:** Ulfrid (tank — damage reduction and reflect; named Brian in older guides, renamed in July 2026), Billy (tank — anti-control, protects key allies, helps vs energy), Joker (carry — executes low-HP targets; named Jester in older posts), Annie (carry — Burning and energy damage), Nicole (support — healing, AoE damage, Warlock buffs).

Warriors are the most complete faction (two tanks, a support, two carries). Rangers are a strong PvP faction built around pressuring dangerous targets. Warlocks are powerful but unfold late — especially once Nicole arrives.

### Overall UR value tiers {#dtf-overall-tiers}

This is a practical value ranking — how often a hero lands in working squads — not a duel simulator:

- **S:** Arthur, Daskal, Marlena, Red Lady, Cynthia, Harper, Shadow.
- **A+:** Bell, Nicole, Ulfrid, Annie, Louis.
- **A:** Zoya, Joker, Billy.

Why the S-tier: Arthur is the most buildable frontline — if the front dies fast, the backline never deals damage. Daskal next to Arthur makes the front extremely dense. Marlena brings energy damage that changes the tempo of a fight in Warrior and hybrid energy comps alike. Red Lady pressures the enemy's main damage dealers. Cynthia pairs with Marlena and Red Lady in strong hybrids. Harper rarely tops damage screens but can be the key piece inside a Warrior squad. Shadow wins fights by breaking the opponent's plan — defense shred plus anti-energy tech.

A+ and A are not weak heroes — they are system heroes. Bell is excellent inside Rangers and weaker outside them. Nicole is near S-tier inside Warlocks but late — never build a starting route around her. Ulfrid is a strong Warlock tank, more faction-bound than Arthur or Daskal. Annie wants Warlock synergy, not a random mix. Louis is a good Ranger tank but less universal than Arthur. Zoya usually plays second Warrior DPS rather than account protagonist. Joker needs a team that softens targets fast. Billy answers control and energy damage but is situational.

![Overall UR hero tier list with Arthur, Marlena, Red Lady and Shadow in S-tier](/img/dtf/tier-list/05.jpg)

### PvP vs PvE reads {#dtf-pvp-pve-reads}

**PvP S:** Arthur, Daskal, Marlena, Red Lady, Cynthia, Shadow, Harper. **A+:** Louis, Ulfrid, Bell, Nicole, Annie. **A:** Joker, Zoya, Billy. Red Lady and Shadow rate highest here: Red Lady deletes high-ATK targets, Shadow shreds defense and disrupts energy squads. Arthur + Daskal is the most reliable front — they buy time rather than win fights. Harper slots in as part of the Warrior core (Arthur + Daskal + Marlena).

![PvP hero tier list with tanks, carries and supports](/img/dtf/tier-list/06.jpg)

**PvE S:** Arthur, Marlena, Daskal, Harper, Nicole. **A+:** Cynthia, Zoya, Annie, Bell, Ulfrid, Louis. **A:** Red Lady, Shadow, Joker, Billy. Nicole climbs because healing and support win long fights; Red Lady drops because pressuring high-ATK targets and stuns matter less against bosses. Arthur, Daskal and Marlena stay high in both modes — frontline and stable damage are needed everywhere. Never copy a PvP squad into PvE blindly: check what the event demands (faction, damage type, survivability over burst).

![PvE hero tier list for bosses and long fights](/img/dtf/tier-list/07.jpg)

### F2P and low-spend priorities {#dtf-f2p-priorities}

The question is not "who is strongest" but "who can I realistically build" — a UR without fragments and stars caps out fast:

- **Very high:** Arthur, Marlena, Daskal, Harper, Cynthia.
- **High:** Red Lady, Shadow, Ulfrid, Annie.
- **Mid/late:** Bell, Louis, Nicole, Zoya, Joker, Billy.

Arthur is the default starter tank. Marlena is the best low-spend damage boost. Daskal is the second front. Harper completes Warriors. Cynthia opens energy hybrids. Lower-rarity heroes bridge the gap: Bella as second front, Celia for PvE and farming, Claire as transitional damage/support — a bridge, not the destination, so don't sink rare resources into them as if they stay forever. The classic F2P mistake is leveling everyone: build one working five and upgrade it slot by slot.

### Best 5/5 squads {#dtf-best-5-5}

**Warrior 5/5 — the most complete faction squad.** Arthur, Daskal, Harper, Marlena, Zoya. Front: Arthur + Daskal. Backline: Marlena + Harper + Zoya. Two tanks, a support and two damage dealers, so the full +20% bonus never breaks the comp. Stable and self-explanatory; if Zoya or Harper is underbuilt, a 4+1 with Cynthia or Red Lady can be better.

![Best Warrior squad: Arthur, Daskal, Harper, Marlena and Zoya](/img/dtf/tier-list/09.jpg)

**Ranger 5/5 — the PvP squad.** Shadow, Louis, Bell, Red Lady, Cynthia. Front: Shadow + Louis. Backline: Red Lady + Bell + Cynthia. Red Lady pressures dangerous targets, Cynthia adds damage, Bell supports, the front holds against energy threats. Strong tempo and control, full bonus — but expensive and late, not a beginner path.

![Ranger squad: Shadow, Louis, Bell, Red Lady and Cynthia](/img/dtf/tier-list/10.jpg)

**Warlock 5/5 — the late system squad.** Ulfrid, Billy, Nicole, Annie, Joker. Front: Ulfrid + Billy. Backline: Annie + Nicole + Joker. Good internal synergy (Burning, healing, executes), but Nicole and Billy are late — without Nicole the comp loses much of its point. Not a first goal for new players.

![Warlock squad: Ulfrid, Billy, Nicole, Annie and Joker](/img/dtf/tier-list/11.jpg)

### Best 4+1 and 3+2 squads {#dtf-hybrid-squads}

4+1 works when a strong faction core plus one outsider beats a weak fifth same-faction hero:

- **Wall of Death (4 Warriors + Cynthia, +15%).** Arthur, Daskal, Harper, Marlena, Cynthia. One of the most reliable comps in the game: the Warrior front and core plus Cynthia's energy pressure. If your Cynthia out-builds your Zoya, the lost 5% bonus pays for itself.

![Energy squad: Arthur, Daskal, Marlena, Cynthia and Red Lady](/img/dtf/tier-list/12.jpg)

- **Warrior core + Red Lady (4W + 1R, +15%).** Arthur, Daskal, Harper, Marlena, Red Lady. Takes the Warrior base and adds Red Lady's PvP pressure on high-ATK targets — ideal against backline-carry teams. She gets no Warrior buffs, so she must be built enough to justify the slot.

3+2 (+10%) is the practical hybrid format for strong, already-built heroes:

- **Pure Energy Domination.** Arthur, Daskal, Marlena, Cynthia, Red Lady (3W + 2R). Aggressive energy burst for PvP and fast fights; weaker in long fights if the front can't hold without a classic support.
- **Control + Collapse.** Arthur, Daskal, Marlena, Shadow, Cynthia (3W + 2R). Shadow adds defense shred and anti-energy control so Marlena and Cynthia convert the window into damage — better against dense teams that survive the first burst, with less pinpoint pressure than the Red Lady version.
- **Warlock Pressure.** Ulfrid, Nicole, Annie, Arthur, Marlena (3 Warlocks + 2 Warriors). A transitional Warlock comp for accounts already building that direction; Nicole shines more with more Warlocks around her.

A hybrid without an ideal bonus is fine when it answers a specific task (e.g. Arthur + Louis front with Marlena, Red Lady and Joker on cleanup duty). It is not a strategy when it is just five favorite URs with no roles covered. Suggested build order from scratch: Arthur → Marlena → Daskal → Harper → Cynthia or Red Lady (stable energy DPS vs PvP pressure) → Shadow → Nicole. Safest default: Warrior 5/5. Aggressive PvP: Arthur + Daskal + Marlena + Cynthia + Red Lady. Control PvP: Arthur + Daskal + Marlena + Shadow + Cynthia. Late Warlock: Ulfrid + Billy + Nicole + Annie + Joker.
