神殿 holds the plot of the three faction statues and serves as the way in to each of them. While the Warrior, Warlock or レンジャー像 is not yet built, a request for it arrives at the 神殿. The building is available from the start of the game and goes up to level 1. Only one can be built.

|---|---|
| Unlocks at | Sanctuary level 1 |
| How many can be built | 1 |
| Max level | 1 |

## Levels and cost

Reference levels of the building: what it gives, how much Might it brings and what the next step costs.

| Level | Might | Cost | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 500 | Grain 12, Timber 9 | under a minute |

## Full upgrade

Taking the building from level 1 to 1 costs Grain 12, Timber 9. Pure build time without speed-ups: under a minute.

This building gives no stat bonus: it unlocks an option rather than percentages.

## How to use it

The 神殿 costs 12 Grain and 9 Timber and goes up in seconds, so at Sanctuary level 1 it competes neither for the queue nor for the thin opening-day stock. Everything else unlocked at that level drags on: the Soldier's Rest needs 299 days 4 hours, the Sanctuary itself 398 days 20 hours, a 農場 49 days 20 hours per copy.

The 神殿 brings 500 Might for 21 resources, and nothing in the city is cheaper: the 民家 gives 100 for 840 resources, while the 領主彫像 gives nothing at all for the same 12 Grain and 9 Timber. Together with the 栄光の講堂 the 神殿 delivers 1,000 Might on day one. The same 500 comes from the 個人厩舎 and the ギルド厩舎, yet both unlock only at Sanctuary level 11, ten levels after the 神殿.

After that the 神殿 serves as a plot. The first faction statue unlocks at Sanctuary level 7, the second at level 11, the third at level 12. Each asks 299 days 4 hours of full construction and carries around 272,500 Might, and all three together 897 days, so the plot is not filled within one season.

Nothing here needs planning: the 神殿 has one level, no upgrades, and its 21 resources are spent once. From level 20 the statues open the Honor levels, and the plot stops being only a temple.
