Bosses come in three kinds and work in different ways. The Abyss boss and the weekend boss reward a matching faction, so the squad is chosen for the boss, while the alliance boss counts the damage of everyone who joins.

## Abyss boss

Each Abyss boss buffs one faction, which settles the choice of squad: a mismatched faction loses half its damage. The table lists the bonus of each boss and the attempts it allows.

| Bonus | Attempts |
|---|---|
| Ranger Hero DMG +50% | 10 |
| Warlock Hero DMG +50% | 10 |
| Warrior Hero DMG +50% | 10 |

## Weekend boss

Same faction bonuses, but only 3 attacks instead of ten.

## Alliance boss

Faction plays no part against the alliance boss; the combined damage of the members does. There are 12 levels, opened by server age: level three on day 7, level four on day 14, then one a week. Personal damage decides how many reward pulls a player collects, and the table shows the thresholds.

| Damage | Reward slots |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2,000,000 | 2 |
| 4,000,000 | 3 |
| 6,000,000 | 4 |
| 8,000,000 | 5 |
| 10,000,000 | 6 |

The first threshold is nominal and every later step comes after another two million damage. Hitting the boss is therefore worth doing even with a weak squad, since one reward pull follows from any hit at all and the rest is a question of investment.

## Hunt War

A separate alliance event: 5 monster waves plus a boss, across 9 difficulty levels.

The key detail about difficulty: the reward is identical on all nine levels. Both the shown payout (500 alliance coins per wave, 2,500 for the boss) and the hidden drop set match; only monster damage grows, from 10,000 to 30,000. There is therefore no reason to pick a harder level than the alliance can clear with confidence.
