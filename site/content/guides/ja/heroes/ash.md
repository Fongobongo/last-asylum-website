## Profile

[Hero] Possesses exceptional shooting skills. Acquire her after successful treatment at the clinic.

## Why she matters

Role: A ranger DPS who strengthens her own kind, as the passive Focus adds 33% monster damage for two rangers at 50 stars. With no stars and no skill levels the bonus is 12.3%, and it goes to the two highest-attack rangers in the squad, so アッシュ pays off specifically in a ranger line-up. Her own blow, Eagle Spirit Hunt, lands 2,798.4% of ATK on a single target.

Worth investing: アッシュ ranks ninth of sixteen DPS heroes by maximum Might (685,873) and sits in tier B. She belongs to the best ranger squad built without UR heroes, so early investment pays off, and once シンシア or レッドレディ opens up the place in the first five goes to them.

## How to play

アッシュ carries 685,873 Might, ninth of sixteen and 80 ahead of ベスター. Eagle Spirit Hunt lands 2,798.4% on a single target, the largest ultimate among the SSR heroes of the role, where クレア manages 2,266.29% and グリンウォルド 2,044.65%. Area damage is what she lacks in return, and One Stone Two Birds strikes two random targets.

SSR heroes carry no server age condition, so アッシュ is available from day one. She holds her place while the UR rangers are out of reach: シンシア arrives on server day 7 and レッドレディ on day 35. The passive Focus strengthens the two rangers with the highest ATK, so part of its value is lost in a mixed line-up.

Her ceiling rests on levels and stars alone, as SSR heroes get neither awakening nor a weapon: 961,611 HP and 14,695 ATK, of which stars give 213,663 and 3,264. Focus stands at 24% until the twentieth star, and from there three steps of 3% at the twentieth, forty-fifth and fiftieth star carry it to 33%.

In a squad without UR heroes アッシュ stands with ステラ, グリフィス, グリンウォルド and ハスター: 7.7M survivability and 1 effectiveness at a +10% bonus. She is more useful alongside グリフィス, ベスター, ハスター and ルイス: the warlock ハスター does not stop the four rangers from earning a +15% bonus, and the five reach 16.8M of survivability and 5 effectiveness.
